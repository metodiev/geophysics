# Exercises — 20-06-01 Particle Motion Analysis and Polarisation Attributes

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-particle-motion-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Particle Motion](../01-particle-motion.md). All models are **SYNTHETIC**
teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A wave packet has covariance eigenvalues $9.0$, $1.0$ and $0.0$ (in
arbitrary amplitude units). Compute the rectilinearity and planarity, and name
the most likely kind of motion.

**★2.** A principal eigenvector is $(0.3536,\ 0.3536,\ 0.8660)$ in the
(north, east, up) frame. Compute the azimuth and the incidence from vertical, and
state the range of the azimuth.

**★3.** A station records a perfectly circular horizontal particle motion.
State the rectilinearity, the planarity, and what the azimuth analysis returns.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A rectilinear arrival is recorded with an amplitude SNR of 4. Estimate
the azimuth standard deviation in degrees, state the order-of-magnitude
assumption behind the estimate, and compare it with the demonstration's measured
value.

**★★5.** Derive the rectilinearity of an elliptical particle motion with axial
ratio $b/a = 0.5$, and state what the result says about using $R$ to distinguish
a Rayleigh wave from a body wave.

**★★6.** A three-component sensor is installed rotated by $+15^\circ$ relative to
north. An arrival has a true back-azimuth of $60^\circ$. What will the analysis
report, and can it be corrected without external information?

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a polarisation-based automatic phase detector for a single
three-component station. State the features, the decision rule, the window
length, and two failure modes with the measurement that would reveal each. A
specification that could not be executed is a failed answer.

**★★★8.** A window returns a rectilinearity of $0.62$ and a planarity of $0.99$.
List the candidate explanations in order of likelihood, and design a measurement
that distinguishes the top two.
