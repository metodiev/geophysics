# Exercises — 23-06-05 Laboratory Magnetic Susceptibility and Remanence

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./05-magnetic-lab-measurements-solutions.md). The lesson is
[05 Magnetic Laboratory Measurements](../05-magnetic-lab-measurements.md). All values are
**SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A specimen has $\kappa = 2.5\times10^{-3}$ (SI) and $\rho = 2700$ kg/m³. Compute the
mass-specific SI susceptibility, $\kappa_{\text{cgs}}$ and $\chi_{\text{cgs,mass}}$, and
state the error made by omitting the $4\pi$.

**★2.** With a suppression field $H_s = 100$ A/m, compute $\kappa_{\text{app}}/\kappa_0$ at
measuring amplitudes of 25, 100 and 400 A/m.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** An IRM acquisition curve has $H_{1/2} = 100$ mT and $\sigma = \ln(10)/2$. Compute
IRM/SIRM at 30, 100, 300 and 1000 mT, and state what it would mean if 300 mT gave 0.5
rather than your computed value.

**★★4.** Two specimens have the same SIRM of 1.10 Am²/kg, with ARM values of 0.11 and
0.022 Am²/kg. Compute both ARM/SIRM ratios, state which specimen is the finer-grained and
why, and state which of the two would dominate a palaeomagnetic signal.

**★★5.** A holder has an equivalent volume of 0.30 cm³. Compute the apparent/true
susceptibility ratio for specimen volumes of 1.0, 5.0 and 20.0 cm³, and the minimum volume
for a 2 % tolerance.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** You are given 20 small chips of drill core (0.4–1.5 cm³ each, total mass 45 g) and
must report the magnetic mineralogy: which minerals carry remanence, their relative
proportions, and the grain-size population. A 1 % susceptibility tolerance and a 2 %
remanence tolerance are requested.

Design the programme: the measurements, the order they are made in, the corrections
applied, the volumes and the specimen holders; show with arithmetic that the requested
tolerances **cannot** be met for the chips and state the tolerances that can be met; state
what the *ratios* can still deliver under those tolerances; and state one claim about the
mineralogy that the programme cannot support.
