# Exercises — 23-06-07 Rock Mechanics Testing: Strength, Friction, Fracture

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./07-rock-mechanics-lab-solutions.md). The lesson is
[07 Rock Mechanics Testing](../07-rock-mechanics-lab.md). All values are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A uniaxial record gives 25 MPa at an axial strain of $0.8\times10^{-3}$. Compute the
secant modulus, then the apparent modulus for a machine stiffness of 4000 kN/mm with
$A = 1.13\times10^{-3}$ m² and $L = 0.080$ m, and state the sign of the error.

**★2.** Compute the stress ratio $\sigma_1/\sigma_3$ required to reactivate a critically
oriented fault with $\mu = 0.6$, and state what happens to that requirement when the pore
pressure rises.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** Triaxial peak strengths are 80, 130 and 200 MPa at confining pressures of 5, 25 and
60 MPa. Fit $\sigma_1 = \mathrm{UCS} + q\sigma_3$, then compute $q$, the friction angle
$\phi$, the UCS and the cohesion $c$. Report the residual at each point.

**★★4.** Using Byerlee's law, compute $\tau$ and $\tau/\sigma_n$ at effective normal stresses
of 30 and 500 MPa, and state which branch of the law applies in each case.

**★★5.** A specimen is tested at 5× the size of a reference specimen and at 20× the loading
rate, with $a\sigma_{\text{eff}} = 0.5$ MPa. Compute both effects and state which is larger
and by how much.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** A CO₂ storage project needs the Mohr-Coulomb parameters and the reactivation
potential of a caprock at 2 km depth, for a reservoir pressure increase of 5 MPa. The
available core is 25 mm diameter; the in-situ effective vertical stress is about 30 MPa; the
reservoir will be depleted by 10 MPa before injection.

Design the test programme: the specimen geometry and the confinement range, with the
argument for both; the number of tests and how the scatter is handled; the machine and the
corrections applied; the friction and reactivation analysis with the pore-pressure scenarios;
the uncertainty budget for $c$ and $\phi$; and the two statements the programme cannot
support.
