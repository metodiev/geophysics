# Exercises — 23-06-06 Thermal Conductivity, Diffusivity and Specific Heat

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./06-thermal-lab-measurements-solutions.md). The lesson is
[06 Thermal Laboratory Measurements](../06-thermal-lab-measurements.md). All values are
**SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A divided bar has a reference of 2.00 W/(m·K) with $\Delta x = 12.0$ mm and
$\Delta T = 5.0$ K. The specimen is 20.0 mm long with $\Delta T = 7.5$ K. Compute the flux
and the specimen's conductivity, then the $K$ error caused by a 0.5 K error in the
specimen's temperature drop.

**★2.** For $K(T) = 2.50 - 2.0\times10^{-3}T$ (T in °C), compute $K$ at 0, 100 and 300 °C
and state the percentage change at each.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** A specimen of 0.150 kg absorbs 95.0 J and rises 2.10 K. Compute $c_p$; with
$\rho = 2700$ kg/m³ and $K = 2.30$ W/(m·K), compute $\alpha$ and $e$. Then compute the
combined relative uncertainty in $\alpha$ for 1 % in $K$, 0.2 % in $\rho$ and 1.428 % in
$c_p$.

**★★4.** Compute the penetration factor $\mathrm{erfc}(z/2\sqrt{\alpha t})$ and the
temperature perturbation for a 4.0 K surface step at 150 m after 200 years and after
1000 years, with $\alpha = 1.0\times10^{-6}$ m²/s. State which of the two is a heat-flow
contamination and which is a climate signal.

**★★5.** For $K_{\text{matrix}} = 4.0$ W/(m·K), $K_{\text{fluid}} = 0.6$ W/(m·K) and
$\phi = 0.15$, compute the arithmetic, geometric and harmonic models, the spread between
the bounds, and the ratio of the spread to the porosity-induced departure from the matrix.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** A geothermal project needs the heat flow of a 2.5 km sedimentary section to 5 %
(95 %), from 12 core plugs and a temperature log with 0.02 K resolution at 1 m spacing.

Design the programme: the conductivity measurement (method, specimen geometry, temperature
range and how the contact term is eliminated); how many specimens and why; the $c_p$ and
$\alpha$ requirements and whether they are needed; the temperature-gradient determination
and its uncertainty; the full budget for the heat flow; the effect of the temperature
dependence of $K$; and the one statement the programme cannot support.
