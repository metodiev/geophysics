# Exercises — 23-06-03 Laboratory Density, Porosity and Permeability Measurement

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-density-porosity-lab-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Density, Porosity and Permeability](../03-density-porosity-lab.md). All values are
**SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A specimen has $m_{\text{dry}} = 145.20$ g, $m_{\text{coated}} = 145.65$ g and
$m_{\text{coated,imm}} = 83.10$ g, with $\rho_{\text{par}} = 0.900$ and
$\rho_w = 0.998$ g/cm³. Compute the film volume, the corrected bulk volume, the bulk
density, and the density the same masses would give if the film were ignored.

**★2.** A pycnometer has $V_c = 80.00$ cm³ and $V_e = 40.00$ cm³ and is run from a gauge
pressure of 120.0 kPa to 75.0 kPa. Compute the solid volume and $\mathrm{d}V/\mathrm{d}p_2$
at that operating point.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** With a grain volume of 52.0 cm³, a bulk volume of 65.0 cm³ and a dry mass of
137.8 g, compute the grain density, the bulk density, the porosity by both forms, and the
mass of water needed to saturate the specimen ($\rho_w = 0.998$ g/cm³).

**★★4.** Gas permeability is 3.2 mD at a mean pressure of 0.4 MPa and 1.6 mD at
1.6 MPa. Fit $K_L$ and $b$, state the liquid-equivalent permeability, and state whether
two points are enough for the extrapolation to be trusted.

**★★5.** A steady-state test uses $A = 9.50$ cm², $L = 0.060$ m, $\Delta p = 0.35$ MPa and
water at $\mu = 1.000$ mPa·s, and measures $Q = 1.20$ mm³/s. Compute the permeability in
m² and mD, then compute the combined relative uncertainty for relative uncertainties of
0.5 % in $Q$, 0.1 % in $L$, 0.2 % in $A$, 3.0 % in $\Delta p$ and 0.5 % in $\mu$, and state
which term dominates.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** A tight-gas core-plug programme must report porosity to ±0.3 porosity points
(95 %) and liquid-equivalent permeability to ±5 % (95 %) for 12 plugs.

Design the measurement programme: the route for the bulk volume and why; the pycnometer
operating point and the pressure-transducer resolution it requires; the Klinkenberg
measurement plan with the number of mean pressures and the pressures themselves; the
steady-state or transient decision for the permeability with the flow rate it implies;
the uncertainty budget showing the two targets are met; the standard checks at each stage;
and one statement the programme will not be able to defend.
