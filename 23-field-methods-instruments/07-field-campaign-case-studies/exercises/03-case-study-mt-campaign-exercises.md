# Exercises — 23-07-03 Case Study: A Magnetotelluric Campaign

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-case-study-mt-campaign-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Case Study: A Magnetotelluric Campaign](../03-case-study-mt-campaign.md). All values are
**SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the period at which the skin depth equals 5 km for resistivities of 50 and
500 Ω·m, and state how the required band changes between the two.

**★2.** With $\gamma^{2} = 0.95$, compute the number of independent period estimates required
for a 2 % impedance accuracy, and the recording time at $T = 20$ s.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** A remote reference raises the coherence from 0.85 to 0.97. Compute the estimate count
before and after for a 1 % accuracy, the time ratio, and state where the gain is largest.

**★★4.** A campaign has 20 sites at 4.0 days each and 2 crews. Compute the site-days, the
calendar days with and without a 10 % contingency, and the crew-days. Then state what changes
if a remote reference site must run for the whole campaign.

**★★5.** Compute the data volume for 20 sites recording four channels at 250 Hz with four bytes
per sample for 4 days each. Then state which resource (archive, crew time, or recording time)
the arithmetic shows to be scarce, and why.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** You must resolve a 1 km deep conductor below a 200 m thick conductive cover, with a
5 % impedance accuracy in the target band, over a 25-site grid, recording in a region with
moderate cultural noise (coherences of about 0.85 at the long periods).

Produce the plan: the band with the arithmetic for both the cover and the target, and the dead
band's location; the recording time per site from the coherence, with and without a remote
reference; the site ranking procedure; the crew and calendar plan with contingency; the model
parameterisation the band supports; the resolution claim you can defend, with the synthetic
test that supports it; and the two statements the campaign cannot make.
