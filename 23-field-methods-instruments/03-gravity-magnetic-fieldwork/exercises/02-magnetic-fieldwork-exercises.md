# Exercises — 23-03-02 Magnetic Fieldwork: Diurnal Variation, Leveling, Gradients

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-magnetic-fieldwork-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Magnetic Fieldwork](../02-magnetic-fieldwork.md). All values are
**SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** The diurnal variation is
$D(t) = 22\sin(2\pi t/24) + 7\sin(2\pi t/8)$ nT with $t$ in hours. Compute $D$ at
$t = 2$ h and at $t = 4$ h, and state why a straight line through $D(0)$ and
$D(8)$ cannot represent the curve.

**★2.** A two-tie linear correction left an RMS residual of 8.600 nT against an
uncorrected 16.720 nT. Compute the fraction of the variation the correction
removed, and state the looping interval that reduced the residual to 0.278 nT.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** Mis-ties have a mean of 0.000 nT and a standard deviation of 7.5 nT, while
the reading noise is 1.5 nT. State what fraction of a 150 nT anomaly the mis-tie
scatter represents, and what the ratio of scatter to reading noise indicates about
the cause of the mis-ties.

**★★4.** An isolated magnetic anomaly has a half-width of 12 m. Using the sphere
model factor measured in the lesson, estimate the source depth and state the
percentage change in the estimate if the true source were a horizontal cylinder
with a different half-width factor. State two assumptions the estimate makes.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★5.** Design the diurnal and levelling plan for a ground magnetic survey that
must deliver a 2 nT anomaly resolution: state the base-station arrangement, the
looping interval and its justification, the tie-line spacing, the levelling
procedure, the repeatability test, and the storm-day rule. Show the arithmetic.
A plan that could not be executed by a two-person crew is a failed answer.

**★★★6.** Specify an experiment that measures the *heading error* of a vector
magnetometer: state the measurement, the reference, the metric with units, the
number of headings needed, and two causes of apparent heading error that are not
heading error. A specification that could not be executed in the field is a failed
answer.
