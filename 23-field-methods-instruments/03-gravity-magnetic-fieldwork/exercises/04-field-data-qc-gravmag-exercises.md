# Exercises — 23-03-04 Field Data QC for Potential-Field Surveys

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-field-data-qc-gravmag-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 Field Data QC](../04-field-data-qc-gravmag.md). All values are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Sixty loops have a closure RMS of 0.0935 mGal. The 56 loops within the
0.15 mGal tolerance have an RMS of 0.0832 mGal. Compute the RMS of the four loops
that exceed the tolerance, and state whether removing them is justified by the
arithmetic alone.

**★2.** Eight stations are repeated five times each; the per-station standard
deviations are 0.0017, 0.0028, 0.0036, 0.0044, 0.0051, 0.0062, 0.0070 and
0.0081 mGal. Compute the pooled repeatability by pooling variances, and the
standard error of a three-reading station value.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** Twenty-four mis-ties have an RMS of 5.203 nT before a constant-per-line
adjustment and 1.981 nT after; the reading noise is 1.5 nT. Compute the mis-tie
noise expected after a perfect adjustment and state whether the adjustment explains
the data. State the criterion you used.

**★★4.** Airborne cross-over errors have a signed mean of +8.155 nT and a standard
deviation about the mean of 4.099 nT. Compute the RMS, state which part a levelling
adjustment removes, and give the resulting RMS. Then state the diagnostic that would
reveal a level error that is *not* constant.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★5.** Design the QC report for a 12-day ground gravity survey: state the
statistics computed daily and at the end, the tolerance for each with its
justification, the actions each failure triggers, and how the report is archived
with the data. Show the arithmetic for at least two tolerances. A report that could
not be produced by a two-person crew at the end of a field day is a failed answer.

**★★★6.** Specify a study that establishes the *achieved* accuracy of a
potential-field survey independently of its own QC statistics: state the
measurements, the reference, the metric with units, and the two failure modes that
internal QC cannot detect. A specification that could not be executed is a failed
answer.
