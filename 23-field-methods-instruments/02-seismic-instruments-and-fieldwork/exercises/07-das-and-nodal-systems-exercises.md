# Exercises — 23-02-07 Nodal Systems and Distributed Acoustic Sensing in the Field

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./07-das-and-nodal-systems-solutions.md). The lesson is
[07 DAS and Nodal Systems](../07-das-and-nodal-systems.md). All values are
**SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A DAS interrogator uses a 5 m gauge on a fibre where the apparent velocity
along the fibre is $2000\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the first two notch frequencies
and the response at 100 Hz.

**★2.** A fibre is interrogated with a 2 m channel spacing and the same
$v = 2000\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the alias frequency and the channel count per
kilometre. State which of the gauge and the spacing limits the usable band here,
given the answer to ★1.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** A wave has a particle velocity of $1.0\ \mathrm{\mu m/s}$ at 5 Hz and an
apparent velocity along the fibre of $1200\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Compute the particle
displacement amplitude, the strain, the strain rate and the acceleration. State
the unit of each and the constant factor that converts strain rate to
acceleration.

**★★4.** A 2000-node survey runs for 15 days and a node records 40 days
continuously on one battery set. Compute the continuous margin and the margin at a
6 h/day duty cycle. State what the duty cycle costs and one class of survey for
which it is unacceptable.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★5.** Design a DAS acquisition for a borehole microseismic monitoring
programme with a target signal band of 5–200 Hz: choose the gauge length and the
channel spacing, justify each from the notch and alias relations, compute the
channel count for a 1.5 km fibre, state the strain sensitivity you would require
and how you would verify it in the field, and state the two design choices you
cannot make without knowing the formation velocities.

**★★★6.** Specify a field experiment that measures the *installation* transfer
function of a trenched fibre: state the co-located reference, the stimulus, the
metric with units, the frequency band it can constrain, and two things it cannot
determine. A specification that could not be executed in a borehole or a trench is
a failed answer.
