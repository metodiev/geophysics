# Exercises — 23-04-05 Borehole Logging Operations and Tool Calibration

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./05-borehole-fieldwork-solutions.md). The lesson is
[05 Borehole Logging](../05-borehole-fieldwork.md). All values are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the elastic stretch for a 750 N tool at 3000 m on a cable of 12 mm²
effective section and $E = 1.1\times10^{11}$ Pa. Express the answer in metres and in
0.05 m sampling intervals.

**★2.** A tool with a 0.60 m detector logs a 0.15 m bed. Compute the peak response
and the apparent thickness, and state which of the two carries the information about
the true thickness.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** A tool samples at 50 samples/s and the target depth sampling is 0.02 m.
Compute the logging speed in m/s and m/h, and the rig time for a 1500 m hole.

**★★4.** Two calibration readings are 500 → 5.0 and 4500 → 45.0. Compute the gain,
the offset, and the calibrated value of a raw reading of 2000. State what the
calibration assumes between the two points.

**★★5.** A repeat log identifies 15 markers with a picking scatter of 0.12 m. Compute
the standard error of the fitted depth shift, and state whether a shift of 0.25 m
would be significant at three standard errors.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** Design a logging programme for a 1200 m well targeting thin (0.3 m) pay
zones, with the tools available being a natural gamma, a density, a sonic and a
resistivity tool: state the tool order, the logging speeds justified from each
tool's resolution and the rig-time budget, the depth-control and calibration
procedure, the repeat checks, and the two limitations the programme cannot remove.
Show the arithmetic.
