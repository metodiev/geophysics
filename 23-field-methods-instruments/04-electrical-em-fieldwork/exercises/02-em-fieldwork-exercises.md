# Exercises — 23-04-02 EM Fieldwork: Loop, Coil and Grounded-Source Configurations

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-em-fieldwork-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 EM Fieldwork](../02-em-fieldwork.md). All values are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the skin depth at 5 kHz in a 30 Ω·m ground. Then compute the
frequency at which the skin depth in that ground is 25 m. Give the intermediate
value of $\sqrt{\rho/f}$.

**★2.** A coil pair has a separation of 40 m. Compute the induction number at
3 kHz in a 20 Ω·m ground and state whether the low-induction-number approximation
applies. State the working threshold used.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** Compute the time at which the diffusing current system reaches 300 m in a
0.2 S/m ground. Then compute the same time for a 0.05 S/m ground, and state the
factor by which the window must lengthen.

**★★4.** A power line lies 15 m from a planned survey line and its coupling is
40 dB above the target signal. Compute the distance at which the coupling would be
10 dB above the target, and state two alternatives to moving the line.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★5.** Design an EM survey for a target at 150 m depth in a ground whose
resistivity you estimate as 20–100 Ω·m: choose the method (frequency- or
time-domain, grounded or inductive), the frequency range or the time window, the
coil or electrode geometry with its depth-of-exploration justification, the
stacking budget, and the coupling distances from the nearest infrastructure. State
the one quantity you must measure before the design is final and how you would
measure it.

**★★★6.** Specify a procedure that verifies whether a small-loop EMI system is
operating inside its low-induction-number regime at a given site: state the
measurement, the analysis, the metric with units, the number of sites needed, and
one reason the test is conservative. A specification that could not be executed in
the field is a failed answer.
