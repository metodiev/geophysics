# Exercises — 23-04-03 Magnetotelluric Fieldwork: Sites, Remote Reference, Noise

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-mt-fieldwork-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 MT Fieldwork](../03-mt-fieldwork.md). All values are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute $|Z|$ for a 50 Ω·m half-space at 2 Hz. Then compute the apparent
resistivity implied by a measured $|Z| = 0.05$ Ω at 2 Hz. Give the intermediate
$\omega\mu_0$.

**★2.** Compute the coherence required for a 3 % accuracy in $|Z|$ from 50
independent estimates. State the relation used.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** A band's coherence falls from 0.97 to 0.88. Compute the relative number
of independent estimates required at each coherence, relative to 0.99, and the
factor by which the recording time must increase.

**★★4.** A telluric field of 1 mV/km is measured on a 150 m dipole with a 5 µV
electrode-plus-preamplifier noise floor. Compute the signal and the SNR. Then
compute the dipole length that would be needed for a 40 dB SNR at the same field.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★5.** Design an MT site layout and recording plan for a target at 1 s period
over a resistive terrane, 5 km from a railway: state the dipole lengths and their
justification from the expected field and the noise floor, the magnetic sensor
placement, whether a remote reference is required and where it should be, the
recording time you would budget and how the coherence requirement sets it, and the
checks made before leaving the site. Show the arithmetic.

**★★★6.** Specify a test that would reveal whether a site's measured coherence has
been *raised* by a coherent noise source rather than by signal: state the
measurements, the comparison, the metric with units, and what the test cannot
detect. A specification that could not be executed at an MT site is a failed answer.
