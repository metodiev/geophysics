# Exercises — 08-01-07 Rock Magnetism: Remanence, Susceptibility and Magnetic Mineralogy

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./07-rock-magnetism-solutions.md). The lesson is
[07 Rock Magnetism](../07-rock-magnetism.md). All materials and parameters are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

Use $\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{H\,m^{-1}}$,
$k_B = 1.380649\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ and
$\tau_0 = 10^{-9}\ \mathrm{s}$.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A hysteresis loop has $H_c = 1.5\times10^{4}\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$.
Express it as $\mu_0H_c$ in tesla and in microtesla.

**★2.** A rock with $\kappa = 0.02$ sits in a field of $48000\ \mathrm{nT}$.
Compute the magnetising field $H$ in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$, the induced
magnetisation $J$ in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$, and the equivalent flux density
$\mu_0J$ in nT. Give the latter as a percentage of the ambient field.

**★3.** From a loop with $J_s = 5.0\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$ and
$J_{rs} = 1.5\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$, compute $J_{rs}/J_s$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** For the rock and field of ★2, compute the Koenigsberger ratio for
$J_r = 0.8\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$, and state whether the rock is
remanence- or induced-dominated. Then compute the ambient field at which
$Q = 1$ for the same $J_r$ and $\kappa$.

**★★5.** Compute the blocking volume and equivalent diameter at
$T = 650\ \mathrm{K}$ for $K = 3.0\times10^{4}\ \mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$ and a
laboratory observation time $\tau = 100\ \mathrm{s}$. Then compute the same for a
geological observation time of $\tau = 10^{15}\ \mathrm{s}$ and report the ratio
of the two blocking diameters.

**★★6.** A synthetic material has $H_{cr}/H_c = 1.3$ and $J_{rs}/J_s = 0.20$;
another has $2.5$ and $0.90$. For each, compute the ambient field at which the
loop's induced magnetisation would be as large as a remanence of
$J_r = 5\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$ if $\kappa = 0.03$, and say which material you
would expect to show a remanent-controlled anomaly.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a laboratory programme to determine whether a remanence is
thermal or chemical in origin. State the measurements, the order in which they
are made, the diagnostic you will use, and one way the experiment can mislead
you. A specification that could not be executed is a failed answer.

**★★★8.** Show that a rock consisting of a fraction $f$ of a high-coercivity
phase (with $J_{rs}/J_s = 0.9$, $H_c = 5\times10^{4}\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$) and
$1-f$ of a low-coercivity phase (with $J_{rs}/J_s = 0.1$,
$H_c = 5\times10^{3}\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$) can produce a bulk $J_{rs}/J_s$ of
0.5 for at least two different values of $f$. State what this implies for
mineral identification from bulk ratios.
