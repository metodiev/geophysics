# Exercises — 08-01-06 Palaeomagnetism, Reversals and Magnetostratigraphy

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./06-palaeomagnetism-solutions.md). The lesson is
[06 Palaeomagnetism, Reversals and Magnetostratigraphy](../06-palaeomagnetism.md).
All directions and chronologies are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values; the only
standard value used is $a = 6371\ \mathrm{km}$.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Twenty site directions give a vector sum of length $R = 18.5$. Compute
$\hat k$ and $\alpha_{95}$, and check that the argument of `arccos` lies in
$[-1,1]$.

**★2.** Compute the palaeolatitude corresponding to $I = 38^{\circ}$ and to
$I = 43^{\circ}$, and report how much a $5^{\circ}$ inclination error costs at
this latitude.

**★3.** Compute the magnetic colatitude $p$ for $I = 65^{\circ}$, and say what
$p = 0$ and $p = 90^{\circ}$ would mean physically.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Compute the virtual geomagnetic pole for a site at
$\lambda_s = 30^{\circ}$, $\phi_s = 100^{\circ}$ with $D = -10^{\circ}$ and
$I = 45^{\circ}$. Report the pole latitude, the pole longitude and the distance
from the geographic pole.

**★★5.** Using the lesson's table of median $\alpha_{95}$ against the number of
sites, estimate how many sites are needed for $\alpha_{95} = 2^{\circ}$, assuming
the $1/\sqrt N$ scaling. State one reason the estimate is optimistic.

**★★6.** A synthetic chronology of 10 Myr has 50 reversals. Compute the rate in a
single 5 Myr window and in the five 2 Myr windows of the lesson's table. Explain
why the 5 Myr number is not five times more reliable than the 2 Myr numbers.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a palaeomagnetic test of the geocentric axial dipole hypothesis
for a chosen geological period. State the sampling design, the age control, the
statistic, the null and alternative hypotheses, and a result that would falsify
GAD. A specification that could not be executed is a failed answer.

**★★★8.** Derive the $2^{-n}$ coincidence probability for a polarity pattern of
$n$ intervals. Then say how the argument changes if one interval is independently
dated: how many intervals are needed for the same confidence, and what assumption
about the reference chronology does the answer use?
