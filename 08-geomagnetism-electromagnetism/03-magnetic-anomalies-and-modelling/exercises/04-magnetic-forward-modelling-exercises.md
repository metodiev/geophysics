# Exercises — 08-03-04 Forward Modelling Magnetic Bodies

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-magnetic-forward-modelling-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 Magnetic Forward Modelling](../04-magnetic-forward-modelling.md). All models
are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A uniformly charged disk of radius $R$ has surface charge
$\sigma = 1.0$ A m$^{-1}$. Compute the axial field at $h = R$ using the closed
form, and state the value as a fraction of the infinite-sheet limit.

**★2.** A vertical-sided prism is magnetised in a direction with components
$M_x = 4.0$, $M_y = 0$, $M_z = 3.0$ A m$^{-1}$. List the faces that carry
magnetic charge and compute $\sigma_m$ on each.

**★3.** State the two representations of a uniformly magnetised body's field used
in the lesson and say in one sentence why using both is a check rather than a
tautology.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** In the demonstration, the anomaly directly above the centre of the prism
is 920.8 nT from the surface-charge model and 13856.4 nT from a single dipole.
Compute the factor by which the dipole estimate is wrong and state, in one
sentence, what this implies for an inversion that fits dipoles to anomalies
measured above shallow bodies.

**★★5.** The burial-depth table gives peaks of 1168.71 nT (top at 0.5 km),
785.11 nT (1.0 km), 392.16 nT (2.0 km) and 115.95 nT (4.0 km). Compute the ratio
of the peak at 2.0 km to the peak at 0.5 km, and the ratio you would predict from
an inverse-cube law over the same change in depth. Explain the difference.

**★★6.** The half-widths are 1.75, 1.80, 2.00 and 2.65 km at the same four depths.
Compute the change in half-width from 0.5 km to 4.0 km of burial and compare it
with the change in depth. State what this says about using half-width as a depth
estimator.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a numerical experiment to determine the distance, in units of
body width, at which the single-dipole approximation of a prism is accurate to
5%. State the reference model, the profile geometry, and how you would avoid
confusing the quadrature error with the approximation error.

**★★★8.** The volume-dipole route and the surface-charge route differ by 0.096%
of the peak. Explain what this residual measures, and predict how it changes if
the cell grid is refined by a factor of two in each direction.

**★★★9.** A strongly magnetic body has $\chi = 1.0$. Explain why the uniformly
magnetised assumption fails for it, and describe qualitatively how the anomaly
would differ from the uniform-magnetisation prediction at a given moment.
