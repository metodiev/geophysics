# Exercises — 08-03-07 Applications: Mineral Exploration, UXO, Archaeology

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./07-magnetic-uses-solutions.md). The lesson is
[07 Applications](../07-magnetic-uses.md). All models are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the induced magnetisation of a sphere with $\chi = 50$ in a field
of $H_0 = 40$ A m$^{-1}$, using the demagnetising factor
$3\chi/(3+\chi)$. State the value it approaches as $\chi\to\infty$.

**★2.** A steel sphere of radius 4 cm has moment
$m = 3.13\times10^{-2}$ A m$^2$. Compute $\Delta T$ at 1.0 m using
$(\mu_0/4\pi)(2m/r^{3})$ and state whether a sensor with 0.10 nT rms noise and a
$3\sigma$ threshold would detect it.

**★3.** Compute the anomaly half-width of a dipole at 0.30 m depth.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** In the sampling table, a 0.50 m station spacing gives a mean loss of
20.71% and a worst case of 48.19%. Compute the anomaly levels recorded in each
case if the true peak is 97.60 nT, and state the practical implication for a
clearance survey with that spacing.

**★★5.** In the gradiometry table, the field metric falls from 0.690 to 0.926
when a smooth regional field is added, while the gradient metric stays at
0.275. Compute the change in each and explain the difference in one sentence.

**★★6.** A reference anomaly of 200 nT comes from $m = 1.0\times10^{9}$ A m$^2$
at 1.0 km. Compute the moment and the volume (at $M = 5$ A m$^{-1}$) required at
3.0 km.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a clearance survey for targets of 1.0 kg of steel at depths up
to 0.5 m. State the assumed susceptibility and density, the resulting moment, the
required station spacing, the required sensor noise, and the detection threshold
you would specify. Show the arithmetic for each choice.

**★★★8.** An archaeological feature is 3 m across at 0.4 m depth. State whether
the dipole approximation is admissible, justify the answer arithmetically, and
describe how you would model it instead.

**★★★9.** A mineral prospect produces a 400 nT anomaly. Two models fit it
equally: a 0.3 km$^3$ body at 400 m with $M = 8$ A m$^{-1}$, or a 25 km$^3$ body
at 4 km with $M = 1$ A m$^{-1}$. List three independent measurements that would
discriminate them, and state what each would show.
