# Exercises — 08-06-02 Environmental Magnetism and Pollution Mapping

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-environmental-magnetics-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Environmental Magnetism](../02-environmental-magnetics.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values with $\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{H\,m^{-1}}$
exact.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the vertical-field anomaly directly above a sphere of radius
$4\ \mathrm{m}$, susceptibility $\chi=0.02$ (SI), at $12\ \mathrm{m}$ depth, in an
ambient field of $48\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$. Show the moment and the anomaly
separately.

**★2.** Using the scaling of $\frac{2}{3}\chi H(a/d)^3\mu_0$, state by what factor
the anomaly changes when (a) the radius is tripled, (b) the depth is halved,
(c) the susceptibility is divided by five.

**★3.** A survey measures a $10\ \mathrm{nT}$ peak with a $1\ \mathrm{nT}$ noise
floor. State the signal-to-noise and whether the body would still be detectable if
it were one depth-multiple deeper.

## ★★ Applied

**★4.** Derive the maximum detection depth
$d_{\max}=a\left(\frac{2}{3}\chi H\mu_0/B_N\right)^{1/3}$ from the sphere formula,
and compute it for $a=3\ \mathrm{m}$, $\chi=0.01$, $H=48\ \mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$,
$B_N=1\ \mathrm{nT}$.

**★5.** A $20\ \mathrm{nT}$ anomaly has $a/d=0.37$. You have an auger that can
reach $5\ \mathrm{m}$ and a trial pit that can reach $10\ \mathrm{m}$. Which would
settle the depth, and what would you do if neither reached the body?

**★6.** Two soil samples give $\chi=2\times10^{-3}$ and $1.6\times10^{-3}$
$\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}}$ but identical SIRM per unit mass. State what has changed
(concentration or grain size) and what you would measure next.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★7.** Design a susceptibility-based pollution map for a former industrial site.
Specify the normalisation, the sampling design, the number of chemical analyses
needed to establish the proxy, the statistic that would accept or reject the proxy,
and how you would report a cell where the proxy fails.

**★8.** A magnetic map shows a $15\ \mathrm{nT}$ anomaly whose shape is
inconsistent with an induced dipole. List the three most likely explanations, in
order, and state the measurement that distinguishes each.
