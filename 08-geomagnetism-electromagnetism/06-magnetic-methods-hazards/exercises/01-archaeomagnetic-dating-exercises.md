# Exercises — 08-06-01 Archaeomagnetic Dating and Secular Variation Curves

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-archaeomagnetic-dating-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Archaeomagnetic Dating](../01-archaeomagnetic-dating.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values, with $\mu_0=4\pi\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{H\,m^{-1}}$
and the Earth's mean radius $6371\ \mathrm{km}$ used as exact constants.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the geocentric axial dipole inclination at latitudes $20^\circ$,
$37^\circ$ and $55^\circ$ using $\tan I=2\tan\lambda$. Give each answer to three
decimals.

**★2.** A measured inclination of $I=45.0^\circ$ is obtained from a material at a
site whose latitude is $37^\circ$. Compute the difference from the GAD expectation
and state whether it is consistent with no tilt.

**★3.** A curve changes direction at $4^\circ$ per century. A measurement has an
angular uncertainty of $0.5^\circ$. Compute the best achievable age precision and
state why the reported uncertainty will be larger.

## ★★ Applied

**★4.** A measured direction is admitted by the curve over the intervals
$-1200$ to $-1050\ \mathrm{yr\,BP}$ and $-350$ to $-150\ \mathrm{yr\,BP}$. State
what the data determine and list, in increasing order of cost, the evidence that
would distinguish the two.

**★5.** The local curve is offset from the reference curve by $2^\circ$ in
declination, and the direction moves at $2^\circ$ per century at the epoch of
interest. Estimate the age bias and compare it with the bias that a $2^\circ$
offset would produce at an epoch where the direction moves at $8^\circ$ per
century.

**★6.** The site of ★2 is found to lie on a block that has rotated $5^\circ$
clockwise since the material cooled. By how much does the rotation change the
recorded declination, and what does it do to the inferred age?

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★7.** Design a campaign to build a regional secular variation curve for the last
$3000$ years. Specify the material classes you will date, the number of
independently dated reference structures per century, the way you will propagate
their dating uncertainties into the curve's envelope, and the criterion for
declaring the curve usable for a given epoch.

**★8.** A structure yields an archaeomagnetic date of $1500\pm50\ \mathrm{yr\,BP}$,
and the excavator reports it as the construction date of the building. Write the
paragraph you would put in the report to make the claim defensible, including the
two largest uncertainties and the one observation that would most improve it.
