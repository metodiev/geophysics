# Exercises — 08-04-06 Geomagnetic Depth Sounding and Long-Period Induction

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./06-geomagnetic-depth-sounding-solutions.md). The lesson is
[06 Geomagnetic Depth Sounding](../06-geomagnetic-depth-sounding.md). All models
are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** For a $10\ \Omega$m half-space at a period of one day, compute
$\delta$, $\left|C\right|$ and $\mathrm{Re}\,C$ and state the two exact relations
you used.

**★2.** Compute the skin depth for $\rho = 100\ \Omega$m at $T = 1$ hour and at
$T = 100$ days, and state the depth range that band covers.

**★3.** State what a non-zero tipper means over an otherwise layered Earth, and
give two distinct physical causes.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** For a source of width 3000 km at a 1-day period in a $10\ \Omega$m
mantle, compute $k$, $\delta$ and $k\delta$, and decide whether a plane-wave
interpretation is justified.

**★★5.** Two models have the same asthenospheric conductance of 10000 S: 10
$\Omega$m over 100 km, and 20 $\Omega$m over 200 km. The demonstration reports a
maximum $\rho_a$ difference of 21.27%. State whether this supports or contradicts
the claim that "the data constrain the conductance", and explain.

**★★6.** In the mantle model, the phase at 1 hour is $69.91^\circ$ and at 100 days
$45.75^\circ$. State what each value says about the conductivity gradient at the
depth sampled, using the $45^\circ$ rule.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study to separate a mantle conductor caused by melt from one
caused by water dissolved in olivine. State the MT quantities you would measure,
the independent observations you would combine, and the decision rule.

**★★★8.** The demonstration tabulates $k\delta$ for three source widths at two
periods. Choose the periods at which a 5000 km source permits a plane-wave
interpretation at $\rho = 10\ \Omega$m, and state the deepest depth you may
therefore claim.
