# Exercises — 05-04-01 Porosity, Density and Velocity Relationships

Difficulty: ★ · ★★ · ★★★.

---

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Derive the density mixing law from a control volume and check the units.

**★2.** State the variability of porosity, grain density, $V_p$ and $V_s$ as
ranges, and say what a single "typical" value would misrepresent.

**★3.** Write the Gassmann $V_s$ prediction (density-only change) and explain
what a violation would mean.

---

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Compute $\rho_b$ for $\phi = 0.18$, $\rho_m = 2650$ kg/m³ and a fluid
of 1020 kg/m³. Then recompute for $S_w = 0.3$ with a hydrocarbon of
250 kg/m³ filling the rest.

**★★5.** Compute $\Delta\rho_b$ when a fluid of 1050 kg/m³ is replaced by one of
180 kg/m³ at $\phi = 0.25$, and state the sign of the resulting density-driven
change in $V_s$ assuming $G$ is unchanged.

**★★6.** Two rocks both have $\rho_b = 2400$ kg/m³. Explain why they may have
$V_p$ differing by a factor of two, naming the property that is doing the work.

**★★7.** A measured $V_s^\text{sat}/V_s^\text{dry} = 0.93$ while the density
ratio predicts 0.96. Quantify the discrepancy and name two candidate mechanisms.

---

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★8.** Build a density–velocity uncertainty budget for a 4-D feasibility
study. State $\phi$, the fluid densities, their uncertainties, and the grain
density uncertainty. Propagate them into $\rho_b$, then state the resulting
uncertainty in acoustic impedance $\rho V_p$ given a stated $V_p$ uncertainty.
Report which input dominates, and the smallest saturation change that would be
detectable at 1% impedance change.

**★★★9.** Using the Gassmann demonstration, compute $V_p$ and $V_s$ for a
porosity sweep with a *fixed* frame law and then with a porosity-dependent frame
law, and explain why the two produce qualitatively different trends in
$\Delta V_p/V_p$. State which is the physically meaningful comparison and why.
