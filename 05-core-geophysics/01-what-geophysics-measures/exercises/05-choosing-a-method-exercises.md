# Exercises — 05-01-05 Choosing a Method: A Decision Framework

Difficulty: ★ · ★★ · ★★★.

---

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** State the four failure modes a feasibility test must probe, and name
the cheapest test for each.

**★2.** Write the contrast-to-noise ratio and explain why the geological noise
term usually dominates.

**★3.** Rewrite this objective as a falsifiable numeric statement: "map the
buried channel."

---

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A target is a tabular body 15 m wide, 4 m thick at 35 m depth, with a
density contrast of 250 kg/m³. Estimate the peak gravity anomaly using the 2-D
line-mass formula and compare it with 0.01 mGal repeatability. State a
conclusion.

**★★5.** The same body is three times more resistive than the host and lies
under 1 m of cover. Electrode spacing must be at most half the target width.
State the spacing, the approximate number of electrodes for a 200 m line, and
whether the density or the resistivity contrast governs the choice.

**★★6.** Compute the magnetic projection factor at inclinations 0°, 30°, 60°,
90° for a vertical magnetisation and state the site latitude at which a
total-field survey of a compact body would be pointless.

**★★7.** An EM target is at 500 m depth in a 0.02 S/m formation. Compute the
minimum period needed for a skin depth of 500 m and state whether a 1 Hz
transmitter is adequate.

---

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★8.** Produce a full method-selection dossier for a stated exploration
problem of your choice (target type, depth, size, contrast range, terrain,
budget). Include: a CNR calculation for two candidate methods; a resolution
calculation for the proposed geometry; an explicit list of every assumption
labelled measured, derived, or assumed; the single falsification test you would
run first; and the decision with its justification. Then state one assumption
whose factor-of-two error would reverse the decision.

**★★★9.** Critique the following plan: "We will run gravity because we already
own the instrument. The target has a density contrast of 500 kg/m³, which is
large, so the anomaly will be easy to see." Address the missing information,
the two calculations that should precede commitment, and what "large contrast"
does and does not imply.
