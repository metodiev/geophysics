# Exercises — 09-05-05 EM for Contaminant Plumes, Salinity and Leachate

Attempt these before the [solutions](./05-em-environmental-solutions.md). Lesson:
[05 EM for Contaminant Plumes, Salinity and Leachate](../05-em-environmental.md).
All models **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute $\sigma_{\text{bulk}}$ for $c = 25$ mol/m³ with
$\Lambda = 0.0126$ S m²/mol, formation factor $F = 6.617$ and surface term
$\sigma_s = 0.0020$ S/m.

**★2.** From the lesson's response table, state the response change produced by a
$10$ m plume at $200$ mol/m³ at $100$ Hz.

**★3.** State in one sentence why the same concentration gives different bulk
conductivities in a sand and in a clay.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Derive the crossover concentration at which the surface term equals the
fluid term, and evaluate it for $F = 10$ and $\sigma_s = 0.005$ S/m with the same
$\Lambda$.

**★★5.** From the detection-threshold table, state the conductance required for a
$5\%$ response at each thickness, and state how it would scale if the threshold
were $1\%$ instead.

**★★6.** A report states "a leachate plume of $10$ mol/m³". Using the measured
thresholds, state the range of thicknesses consistent with that claim at a $5\%$
detection level.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design an environmental EM survey for a $5$ m thick plume at $10$ m
depth targetting $3\%$ detectability. State the band, the station spacing, the
repeat policy and the statistic.

**★★★8.** The lesson's model has no plume geometry, no concentration gradient and
no temperature effect. State which of these would change the *detectability*
conclusion and which would change only its *interpretation*, with a reason for
each.
