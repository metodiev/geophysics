# Exercises — 09-05-01 TEM Sounding for Aquifers and Mineral Targets

Attempt these before the [solutions](./01-tem-sounding-solutions.md). Lesson:
[01 TEM Sounding](../01-tem-sounding.md). All models **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the diffusion depth $\delta = \sqrt{2t/(\mu_0\sigma)}$ for
$\sigma = 0.02$ S/m at $t = 0.1$, $1$ and $10$ ms, with
$\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ H/m.

**★2.** State the frequency corresponding to $t = 1$ ms, and explain in one
sentence why that conversion is exact.

**★3.** For a $50$ m offset, $f = 1000$ Hz and $\sigma = 0.01$ S/m compute the
induction number $\theta = \omega\mu_0\sigma r^{2}/4$ and say whether the
low-induction-number calibration applies.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** The measured response ratio divided by $i\theta$ tends to $-0.984$ at
$\theta = 10^{-4}$. State the limit, and explain what the two channels of a loop
response measure in that regime.

**★★5.** Using the measured median-depth table ($z_{50} = 48.2$, $47.4$, $42.5$,
$33.2$ m at $\delta = 5032.92$, $1591.55$, $503.29$, $159.15$ m), fit
$z_{50} \propto \delta^{p}$ and compare with the demonstration's $p = 0.107$.

**★★6.** A $2$ m layer of $1$ S/m is replaced by a layer of the same conductance
but four times the thickness. Using the measured equivalence table, state the rms
difference in $|H_s|$ and whether a $1\%$ noise level could tell them apart.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a two-offset sounding that separates the $2$ m/1 S/m and
$20$ m/0.1 S/m members of the equivalence family. State the offsets, the
statistic, and the noise level required to make the separation significant.

**★★★8.** The demonstration shows that a normal-incidence (plane-wave)
reflection coefficient is frequency-independent. Derive that statement from
$R = (u - \lambda)/(u + \lambda)$ as $\lambda \to 0$, and state why it rules out
a plane-wave model of a transient sounding.
