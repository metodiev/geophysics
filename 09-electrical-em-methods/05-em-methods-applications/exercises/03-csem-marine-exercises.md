# Exercises — 09-05-03 Marine CSEM for Hydrocarbon and Gas Hydrate Exploration

Attempt these before the [solutions](./03-csem-marine-solutions.md). Lesson:
[03 Marine CSEM](../03-csem-marine.md). All models **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute $\delta = \sqrt{2/(\omega\mu_0\sigma_w)}$ at $f = 0.1$ Hz for
$\sigma_w = 3$ S/m, and the offset at which the water path is down $-40$ dB.

**★2.** From the lesson's MVO table, state the offset at which the reservoir's
signature is largest and its value.

**★3.** Explain in two sentences why marine EM looks for a resistor while mineral
EM looks for a conductor.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Derive the $-20$ dB offset $\delta\ln 10$ from
$\exp(-r/\delta) = 0.1$, and evaluate it at $0.25$ Hz in $500$ m of water.

**★★5.** Using the signature-versus-frequency table at $2$ km, estimate the
frequency that maximises the product of the signature magnitude and the amplitude
attenuation, and state the two approximations you used.

**★★6.** A survey reports a $3\%$ MVO anomaly at $1$ km with a $1\%$ noise level.
Using the measured sensitivity (a factor $200$ in reservoir resistivity changes
the response by less than $0.1\%$ at $2$ km), state what the anomaly does and does
not establish.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a multi-offset survey that constrains a reservoir's conductance
to within $25\%$, stating the offsets, the statistic, and the assumption you are
accepting about the water column.

**★★★8.** The lesson states that the model omits the reflecting sea surface.
Describe what that omission does to a low-frequency, long-offset measurement, and
state what model would be needed to include it.
