# Exercises — 09-01-04 Clay Effects, CEC and Membrane Polarisation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-clay-and-surface-conduction-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 Clay Effects, CEC and Membrane Polarisation](../04-clay-and-surface-conduction.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute $Q_v$ for $\mathrm{CEC} = 8\ \mathrm{meq/100\,g}$,
$\rho_g = 2.65\ \mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$ and $\phi = 0.20$, and state the units.

**★2.** With $\beta = 0.10\ \mathrm{(S\,m^{-1})/(eq\,L^{-1})}$, compute
$\sigma_s$ for the $Q_v$ of ★1.

**★3.** Compute the DC chargeability $m = (\sigma_\infty-\sigma_0)/\sigma_\infty$
for $\sigma_0 = 0.02$ and $\sigma_\infty = 0.025\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A clay-rich sand has $\phi = 0.20$, $\sigma_w = 0.2\
\mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$, $m = 2$, $\mathrm{CEC} = 8\ \mathrm{meq/100\,g}$ and
$\beta = 0.10$. Compute $\sigma$, then the water saturation with $n = 2$ both
with and without the surface correction, and state which is admissible.

**★★5.** For the sample in ★4, a 50% reduction in water saturation is proposed.
Compute the change in the measured conductivity and the change that would be
expected if the surface term did not exist. Explain why the survey may fail to
see the saturation change.

**★★6.** The Debye model of the lesson has $\sigma_0 = 0.05$,
$\sigma_\infty = 0.06\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$ and $f_c = 2$ Hz. Compute
$\tau$, the peak phase frequency, and the phase at $f = 0.01$ Hz. State which of
these numbers a DC resistivity survey needs.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Using the identity
$(2/\pi)\int|\varphi|\,\mathrm{d}\ln f = \ln(\sigma_\infty/\sigma_0)$, compute
the phase-area value for $m = 0.4$ and compare it with $m$. Then state the
largest $m$ for which treating the area as $m$ introduces an error below 10%.

**★★★8.** Design a laboratory experiment to separate the electrolytic and
surface contributions of a clay-bearing sandstone. Specify the fluids, the
salinities, the plots, and the quantitative criterion that would tell you the
measurement has failed to separate them.
