# Exercises — 09-01-02 Archie's Law, Porosity and Saturation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-archies-law-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Archie's Law, Porosity and Saturation](../02-archies-law.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A clean sand has $a = 1$, $m = 2.1$, $\phi = 0.30$. Compute $F$ and,
for $\rho_w = 0.5\ \Omega\,\mathrm{m}$, the saturated resistivity $\rho_0$.

**★2.** For the rock in ★1 with $n = 2.0$, compute $\rho_t$ at
$S_w = 0.5$ and state the units.

**★3.** Invert $F = \phi^{-2}$ for $\phi$ when $F = 40$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Two cores from the same unit give $F = 32$ at $\phi = 0.18$ and
$F = 12$ at $\phi = 0.30$. Solve for $m$ and $a$, showing the arithmetic of both
logarithms.

**★★5.** A measured $F$ is 10% too high because the pore-water resistivity was
taken from a chart rather than measured. Compute the resulting porosity error
for $m = 2.0$ and for $m = 2.5$, and state which is more sensitive.

**★★6.** A sandstone is believed to be at $S_w = 0.35$ with $n = 2.2$. The
resistivity is measured to 3%. Compute the uncertainty in $S_w$ and decide
whether the saturation target of $\pm0.05$ is met.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Show that the geometric model $F = \tau/\phi$ with constant $\tau$ and
Archie's law with $a = 1$ are consistent only if $m = 1$. Then compute the
implied tortuosity for $m = 2$ at $\phi = 0.35$ and at $\phi = 0.08$, and discuss
whether the growing tortuosity is physically defensible.

**★★★8.** Design a laboratory programme that determines $a$, $m$ and $n$ for a
single rock type with stated uncertainties. State how many samples, which
measurements, which fluids, and how you would detect that Archie's law is the
wrong model for one of the samples.
