# Exercises — 09-01-03 Electrolytic, Electronic and Surface Conduction

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-conduction-mechanisms-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Electrolytic, Electronic and Surface Conduction](../03-conduction-mechanisms.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values; the physical constants quoted in
the lesson are exact SI-defined values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Using $\sigma_w = F c(\mu_+ + \mu_-)$ with the constants printed in the
lesson, compute $\sigma_w$ for $c = 0.005\ \mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ and give
$\rho_w$.

**★2.** A sample has $\phi = 0.22$, $m = 2.0$ and $\sigma_w = 0.30\
\mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$. Compute the Archie conductivity and the resistivity.

**★3.** A grain has $\sigma_g = 10^{4}\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$ and the matrix
conductivity is $10^{-6}\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$. Compute the parallel bound for a
grain fraction of 0.8 and state the units.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A sample measures $\sigma = 0.08575\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$ with
$\phi = 0.25$, $\sigma_w = 0.50\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$. Compute the surface
conductivity and the apparent porosity if the surface term is ignored.

**★★5.** For the sample in ★4, the surface term is known only to $\pm30\%$.
Compute the range of porosity that results and compare it with the porosity
range from a $\pm3\%$ measurement error alone.

**★★6.** A synthetic rock contains 4% by volume of connected sulphide
($\sigma_g = 10^{4}\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$) in a matrix of
$10^{-6}\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$, with $\phi = 0.15$ pore fluid at
$0.5\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$. Compute the parallel and series bounds and state
which measurement would help decide between them.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Derive the Hashin–Shtrikman-type tighter bound for a two-phase
mixture with conductivities $\sigma_1 \le \sigma_2$ and volume fractions
$f_1, f_2$, or explain precisely why you cannot, and use the looser bounds you do
have to state the maximum possible error of a conductivity-derived porosity in
the sulphide case of ★6.

**★★★8.** A survey measures a low-resistivity anomaly and a colleague proposes
to estimate the water saturation from it using Archie. Write the decision tree
you would follow, listing each measurement you would demand and the specific
numerical test that would falsify the Archie assumption.
