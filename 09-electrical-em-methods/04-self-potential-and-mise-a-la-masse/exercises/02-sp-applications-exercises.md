# Exercises — 09-04-02 SP for Seepage, Dams, Landfills and Mineralisation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-sp-applications-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 SP Applications](../02-sp-applications.md). All models are **SYNTHETIC**
teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A single-lobe SP anomaly has FWHM $= 80.0$ m. Give the depth it implies
under the point-electrode rule ($3.4641h$) and under the compact-dipole rule
($1.5328h$).

**★2.** Using the lesson's measured detection table, state the moment $IL$ needed
to detect a $160$ m deep compact dipole at $\mathrm{SNR} = 3$ with
$\sigma_n = 1\ \mu$V per station, and the peak anomaly that moment produces.

**★3.** State the metric that constrains the depth of an SP source and the metric
that constrains its strength, and explain in one sentence why neither alone is
sufficient.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Derive $\lVert\mathbf{v}\rVert \propto IL/h^{3/2}$ for the compact
dipole template, stating the two approximations used, and explain why the
measured exponent of the detection law is $1.542$ rather than exactly $1.5$.

**★★5.** A survey reports $20\%$ noise on the peak anomaly and a depth of $40$ m
with no uncertainty. Using the Monte Carlo table, state the missing number and
explain why the median is not the whole answer.

**★★6.** A profile has a $1.70$ mV linear ramp across $400$ m added to a
$21.22$ mV anomaly. By how much does the fitted source current change, and why is
the answer exactly zero in this estimator?

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design an SP survey and its levelling policy to detect a $5$ mV
anomaly of $150$ m width in an area with a $0.01$ mV/m regional trend, with a
budget of two days of field time. Specify the station spacing, the profile
length, the repeat-measurement policy, the levelling function, and the statistic
that would confirm the anomaly is not an artefact of the levelling.

**★★★8.** The lesson measures that one profile rejects a shallow sheet at
$6.93$ noise units but cannot separate a dipole from a short vertical line
($1.04$ against $1.45$). Explain geometrically why the sheet is separable and the
line is not, and state what additional measurement would separate the line from
the dipole.
