# Exercises — 09-04-01 Sources of Self-Potential: Electrokinetic, Electrochemical

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-self-potential-sources-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Sources of Self-Potential](../01-self-potential-sources.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** With $\varepsilon_r = 80.1$, $\zeta = -25.0$ mV, $\eta = 1.0\times10^{-3}$
Pa s and $\sigma = 0.020$ S/m, compute $\partial V/\partial P$ in V/Pa, convert it
to mV per metre of water head, and state the head that produces a $5.0$ mV
anomaly.

**★2.** Compute the standard cell potential of a cell with a
$\mathrm{Cu^{2+}/Cu}$ cathode ($E^{0} = +0.342$ V) and a $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}/Zn}$
anode ($E^{0} = -0.762$ V), and state the sign convention used.

**★3.** For a salinity contrast of one decade, compute the membrane potential for
$t_+ = 0.30$ and for $t_+ = 0.75$, using
$E = \frac{RT}{F}\left(t_+ - t_-\right)\ln 10$ with
$\frac{RT}{F}\ln 10 = 59.159$ mV.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Derive $\mathrm{FWHM} = 2\sqrt{2^{2/3}-1}\,h = 1.5328\,h$ for a point
dipole from $V(x) \propto h/\left(x^{2}+h^{2}\right)^{3/2}$, showing the algebra
that gives $x^{2} = h^{2}\left(2^{2/3}-1\right)$.

**★★5.** A single-lobe SP anomaly has FWHM $= 120.0$ m. Compute the depth it
implies under the point-electrode rule and under the compact-dipole rule, and
state the ratio.

**★★6.** A buried electrode of $1.0$ A is at $60$ m depth in a host of
$50\ \Omega$ m. Compute the surface potential directly above it and at $100$ m
offset, and show that the apparent resistivity is the same at both stations.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a survey that would separate a streaming-potential source from
a redox source when both produce a $15$ mV single-lobe anomaly: state the
additional measurements, the prediction of each mechanism, and the statistic that
decides between them. The design must be executable with the methods of this
module.

**★★★8.** The demonstration fits a three-parameter dipole family and finds an
admitted ridge with $\mathrm{corr}(\text{depth}, \text{length}) = +0.854$. Specify
an additional measurement that would shrink that correlation, and predict the
direction of the shrinkage (whether the admitted set becomes a point or stays a
ridge) with your reasoning.
