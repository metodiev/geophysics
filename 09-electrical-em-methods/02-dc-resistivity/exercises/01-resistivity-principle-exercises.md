# Exercises — 09-02-01 Resistivity Principle and the Four-Electrode Arrangement

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-resistivity-principle-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Resistivity Principle and the Four-Electrode Arrangement](../01-resistivity-principle.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the potential at 3 m from a point source injecting
$0.2\ \mathrm{A}$ into a $75\ \Omega\,\mathrm{m}$ half-space.

**★2.** Compute the geometric factor of a Wenner array with $a = 10$ m, from the
general formula and from $2\pi a$.

**★3.** The array in ★2 reads $\Delta V = 0.5\ \mathrm{V}$ at $I = 0.2\
\mathrm{A}$. Compute $\rho_a$ with units.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A dipole-dipole array has $a = 10$ m and $n = 2$. Compute $k$, and the
$\Delta V$ expected over a $200\ \Omega\,\mathrm{m}$ half-space at
$I = 0.5\ \mathrm{A}$.

**★★5.** The four electrodes of a Wenner array with $a = 20$ m are surveyed to
$\pm2$ cm. Over a uniform half-space, compute the resulting $\rho_a$ error using
the *nominal* geometry; repeat for a two-layer ground where the sensitivity is
$0.26\%$ per centimetre on the inner electrodes. Explain the difference.

**★★6.** A two-electrode measurement uses hemispheres of radius 0.04 m on a
$60\ \Omega\,\mathrm{m}$ half-space with $300\ \Omega$ of contact resistance per
electrode. Compute the measured resistance, the apparent resistivity and the
error factor.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Show that reciprocity holds for the general four-electrode formula,
and state precisely what property of the governing equation it depends on.
Then construct a forward model (in words) that would violate it and explain why
it is physically wrong.

**★★★8.** Design a field procedure that reduces the geometric error of a
four-electrode survey to below 0.2% over a two-layer ground, given that a
centimetre-precision electrode placement is not achievable at depth. Include the
measurement you would record for each electrode and the analysis you would apply
afterwards.
