# Module 00-04 — What Geophysics Is and Who Does It

**Stage:** beginner · **Prerequisites:** [00-02 curriculum map](../02-curriculum-map/README.md)

This module is the **subject introduction**. It defines geophysics as inference
from remote measurement, surveys the subdisciplines by what each one physically
measures and why it works, traces how the field developed, describes the kinds of
employers and roles that use it, and sets out the societal applications that
justify the public investment in it.

No mathematics beyond school level is assumed. The purpose of the module is that
you can read a geophysical claim and know, in outline, what measurement it rests
on and what it cannot establish.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module you will be able to:

1. Explain what a property contrast is and why every geophysical method depends on
   one.
2. Distinguish potential-field methods from wave methods from flux and
   deformation measurements, and predict which is suitable for a stated target.
3. Describe, for each major subdiscipline, the property it senses, the mechanism
   that makes it measurable, and its characteristic blind spot.
4. Place the major advances of the subject in order, and explain why each depended
   on a new measurement capability rather than a new idea alone.
5. Name the categories of employer and role archetype in the field, and explain
   what a professional society provides.
6. Read a hazard or resource statement critically, distinguishing what
   geophysics measures from the decision it informs.

## The chain, one more time

```text
property contrast → field or wave response → measurement
                  → inversion → model + uncertainty
```

Every lesson in the remaining 26 folders elaborates one link of that chain. When
a derivation loses you, return to this sentence and identify which link you are
on. When a claim seems too strong, the weakness is usually at the inversion or
the uncertainty link.

## Honesty note

Lessons in this module describe categories, mechanisms and history. They avoid
figures that depend on a particular compilation or year — salaries, employment
proportions, reserves, loss statistics, sea-level rates. Where such a figure is
needed, the lesson says which body publishes it, so that you can consult the
current source and cite it properly rather than repeating a number of unknown
provenance.

## Mastery checkpoint

You have completed this module when you can, unaided:

- [ ] Explain, in two minutes and without jargon, why a homogeneous Earth would
      be almost invisible to geophysics.
- [ ] Given a target (a shallow aquifer, a deep orebody, a crustal fault), name
      two methods that would sense it and say which contrast each detects.
- [ ] Describe one historical episode in which an instrument, rather than a
      theory, drove the advance.
- [ ] State the difference between what a geophysical measurement establishes and
      what a societal decision requires, for one hazardous or resource
      application.
