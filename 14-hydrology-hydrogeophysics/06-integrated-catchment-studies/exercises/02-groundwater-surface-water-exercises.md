# Exercises — 14-06-02 Groundwater-Surface Water Interaction

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-groundwater-surface-water-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Groundwater-Surface Water Interaction](../02-groundwater-surface-water.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Two piezometers 8 m apart across a streambed read a head difference of
0.060 m with the stream higher than the aquifer, and $K = 2.5\ \mathrm{m\,d^{-1}}$.
Compute the specific discharge, its sign, and the seepage velocity for
$n_e = 0.25$.

**★2.** Compute the thermal skin depth for a diurnal and for a semidiurnal
(solar tide, 12.4 h) period, for
$\kappa = 1.0\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$.

**★3.** A dilution gauging injects 2.00 kg of salt and measures
$\int\Delta C\,\mathrm{d}t = 23641.18\ \mathrm{kg\,s\,m^{-3}}$ over a 1800 s
window. Compute the discharge, and compute what it would be if the true integral
over a longer window were 24889.25.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A temperature profile at 0.15 m and 0.30 m gives an amplitude ratio of
0.7000 and a phase lag of 3.00 h. Using the demonstration's table and the
homogeneous inversion results, state what pair of velocities the two readings
imply and whether they are consistent.

**★★5.** The same reach is repeated in a second year with a silt layer present.
The readings are a ratio of 0.1918 and a lag of 3.9893 h. Explain, with the
numbers from the lesson, why one of these readings cannot be explained by any
homogeneous bed, and what you would do next.

**★★6.** Two dilution gaugings bracket a 100 m reach: 30.00 L/s upstream and
30.07 L/s downstream. The stream is 2 m wide. Compute the exchange flux per unit
bed area, the seepage velocity, and the fractional difference between the two
gaugings. State whether this measurement is defensible and what you would use
instead.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a temperature-monitoring array to estimate a seepage velocity
of $0.1\ \mathrm{m\,d^{-1}}$ to $\pm 30\%$ in a bed whose thermal diffusivity is
unknown to $\pm 20\%$. State the probe depths, the sampling interval, the record
length, and how you would separate the thermal-diffusivity error from the
velocity. Justify each choice with a number.

**★★★8.** A regulator asks you to certify the sign of the exchange (gaining or
losing) at 30 reaches along a river, with no boreholes and one field day per
reach. Propose the cheapest method that can certify the *sign* (not the
magnitude), state its failure modes, and say what fraction of the reaches you
expect to answer.
