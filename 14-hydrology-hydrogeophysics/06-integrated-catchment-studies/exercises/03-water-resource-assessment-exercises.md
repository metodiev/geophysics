# Exercises — 14-06-03 Water Resource Assessment and Sustainable Yield

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-water-resource-assessment-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Water Resource Assessment](../03-water-resource-assessment.md). All models
are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A water-level record shows a seasonal rise of 0.85 m. Compute the
recharge for $S_y = 0.10$ and for $S_y = 0.15$, in mm/a, and state the ratio.

**★2.** With $P = 800$ mm/a, $\mathrm{Cl}_{\text{dep}} = 0.5$ mg/L and
$\mathrm{Cl}_{\text{gw}} = 12.0$ mg/L, compute the chloride mass balance
recharge. Repeat for $\mathrm{Cl}_{\text{gw}} = 8.0$ mg/L and state what a lower
groundwater chloride implies about recharge.

**★3.** For $L = 1500$ m, $T = 200\ \mathrm{m^2\,d^{-1}}$ and $S = 0.10$,
compute the argument $L\sqrt{S/(4Tt)}$ at $t = 7300$ d and, using the lesson's
value $\operatorname{erfc}(0.1963) = 0.7813$, state the fraction of pumpage
captured from the stream at 20 years.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A well pumps $800\ \mathrm{m^3\,d^{-1}}$ at the site of ★3. Compute the
stream depletion and the volume taken from storage at 5 years, and the cumulative
volume captured from the stream over those 5 years (the lesson gives
688872 m³ at $Q = 1000\ \mathrm{m^3\,d^{-1}}$ — scale it).

**★★5.** The transmissivity is uncertain by a factor of two either way. Using
$t_{10} = L^2S/(4T\,\alpha^2)$ with $\alpha = 1.1631$, compute the time to 10%
depletion for $T = 100$ and $T = 400\ \mathrm{m^2\,d^{-1}}$, and state the
implication for a licence written at the median.

**★★6.** The low-flow discharge is $4320\ \mathrm{m^3\,d^{-1}}$ and the tolerance
is 10% of it at a five-year horizon. Compute the permitted pumping for the
nominal 5-year depletion fraction of 0.5788, and for the Monte Carlo 5–95%
fractions of 0.384 and 0.803. State the decision consequence.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a monitoring programme that would detect a breach of the
licence condition of ★6 within one year, naming the instruments, the measurement
frequency, the statistic used to judge compliance, and the false-alarm and
detection characteristics you would accept. Quantify the measurement precision
you need from the numbers you have computed.

**★★★8.** A water-rights hearing asks you to state the sustainable yield of the
idealised aquifer as a single number. Write the answer you would give, in full,
and justify why it contains more than one number and at least one probability.
