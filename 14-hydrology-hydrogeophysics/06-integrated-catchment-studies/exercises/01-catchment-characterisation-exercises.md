# Exercises — 14-06-01 Integrated Catchment Characterisation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-catchment-characterisation-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Integrated Catchment Characterisation](../01-catchment-characterisation.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A SYNTHETIC catchment of $12.0\ \mathrm{km^2}$ has, over one water year,
$P = 807.6$ mm, $Q = 159.2$ mm and $\Delta S = 19.6$ mm. Compute ET, the runoff
coefficient and the evaporation ratio.

**★2.** A water-level record shows a seasonal rise of 0.72 m. Compute the
recharge for $S_y = 0.08$ and for $S_y = 0.12$, and state the ratio.

**★3.** A refraction sounding gives an intercept time of 5.00 ms, a refracted
slope of $0.4000\ \mathrm{ms\,m^{-1}}$ and a direct-branch velocity of 600 m/s.
Compute $v_2$ and the layer thickness.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Using the demonstration's Lyne–Hollick table, state the baseflow index
range for $\alpha \in [0.90, 0.99]$ and design a one-page reporting template for
a BFI that a reader could reproduce.

**★★5.** A catchment of $15.0\ \mathrm{km^2}$ has a mean regolith thickness of
3.2 m known to $\pm 0.10$ m, and a specific yield of 0.08 known to $\pm 0.03$.
Compute the storage volume, each uncertainty contribution, and the fractional
uncertainty of the volume.

**★★6.** In the demonstration's recession family the $b = 1.50$ member has
$Q_0 = 1.496\ \mathrm{mm\,d^{-1}}$ and $a = 3.7305\times10^{-2}$. Compute the
implied storage from $S = Q^{2-b}/[a(2-b)]$ and compare it with the $b = 1.00$
value of 53.56 mm. State what changes in the aquifer would make $b = 1.5$ the
better description.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a field campaign that would determine the catchment storage
volume to $10\%$ at one standard deviation. Work backwards from the error budget
of the lesson: state the accuracy needed in the specific yield and in the
thickness, the methods that could deliver each, and the number of sites. A
specification that could not be executed is a failed answer.

**★★★8.** The lesson argues that an integrated characterisation is limited by
its least-identified link. Write a short critique of a study that reports a
storage volume to three significant figures, naming the three quantities that
would have to be independently measured to justify the precision, and the
observation that would be most diagnostic if only one were available.
