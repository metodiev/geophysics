# Exercises — 14-04-04 Acid Mine Drainage and Mining Environmental Geophysics

Attempt before the [solutions](./04-acid-mine-drainage-solutions.md). Lesson:
[04 Acid Mine Drainage and Mining Environmental Geophysics](../04-acid-mine-drainage.md).
All models **SYNTHETIC**; no real mine or site is referenced. Use standard atomic
weights $\mathrm{Fe} = 55.845$, $\mathrm{S} = 32.060$, $\mathrm{O} = 15.999$,
$\mathrm{H} = 1.008$, $\mathrm{Ca} = 40.078$, $\mathrm{C} = 12.011$.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Write the idealised oxidation reaction for pyrite and verify the atom and
charge balance by counting.

**★2.** Compute $M(\mathrm{FeS_2})$ and $M(\mathrm{SO_4})$ from the atomic
weights, then the sulphate yield per kilogram of pyrite.

**★3.** Compute the acidity yield in $\mathrm{mol\,H^{+}}$ per kilogram of pyrite
and the calcium-carbonate equivalent mass needed to neutralise it.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A synthetic waste contains $0.4\%$ pyrite by mass at an in-place
density of $1800\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. **(a)** Compute the pyrite mass and the
sulphate mass per cubic metre. **(b)** Compute the sulphate concentration in the
percolating water for a percolation ratio of $3.0\ \mathrm{m^{3}}$ per
$\mathrm{m^{3}}$. **(c)** State why the answer is an upper bound.

**★★5.** Using $\sigma_w = 0.050 + 2.0\times10^{-5}C$ (with $C$ in
$\mathrm{mg\,L^{-1}}$) and $\phi^{m} = 0.09$, compute the bulk resistivity at the
background, at $1000\ \mathrm{mg\,L^{-1}}$ and at the concentration of ★★4(b).
State the fractional change at each.

**★★6.** The conductive front travels at $v = 0.040\ \mathrm{m\,day^{-1}}$ with
$\alpha_L = 2.0\ \mathrm{m}$. **(a)** Compute the arrival time at a monitoring
station $40\ \mathrm{m}$ down-gradient. **(b)** Compute the 10–90% transition
width at that time. **(c)** State whether electrode spacings of $5\ \mathrm{m}$
resolve the front, with a number.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a closure-monitoring programme for an idealised waste-rock
facility. **(a)** State the three questions the programme must answer and the
measurement that answers each. **(b)** Explain why a conductivity map cannot
measure the remaining sulphide mass, and what could. **(c)** State the single
largest source of uncertainty in a predicted discharge concentration and how it
would be reduced.

**★★★8.** The self-potential model
$V(x) = \rho I/(2\pi\sqrt{x^{2}+d^{2}})$ is a compact source in a uniform
half-space. **(a)** Show that $\rho$ and $I$ are not separable. **(b)** Explain
why a *distributed* redox zone produces a broader anomaly than the point-source
model, and how that biases a depth estimate based on the half-width.
**(c)** Propose a measurement that distinguishes a deep compact source from a
shallow distributed one, and state the resolution limit.
