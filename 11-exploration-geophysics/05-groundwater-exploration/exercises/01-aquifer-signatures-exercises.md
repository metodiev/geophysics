# Exercises — 11-05-01 Geophysical Signatures of Aquifers and Saline Interfaces

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-aquifer-signatures-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Geophysical Signatures of Aquifers and Saline Interfaces](../01-aquifer-signatures.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A fresh-water head of $2.0\ \mathrm{m}$ above sea level overlies sea
water with $\rho_s = 1025\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Compute the sharp-interface
depth with $\rho_f = 1000\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, and state the ratio $z/h_f$.

**★2.** Using Archie's law with $a = 1$, $m = 2$, $n = 2$, compute the bulk
conductivity of a fully saturated sand with $\phi = 0.25$ and pore water of
$\sigma_w = 0.2\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$. Then compute it again for $S_w = 0.6$.

**★3.** A saturated layer has $\phi = 0.28$. Compute the density change on
saturation, $\Delta\rho = \phi(\rho_w-\rho_{\text{air}})$ with
$\rho_w = 1000$ and $\rho_{\text{air}} = 1.2\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, in both
$\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and $\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A microgravity survey over an alluvial aquifer resolves $8\
\mu\mathrm{Gal}$. Using the Bouguer slab formula in survey units, compute the
minimum fully saturated thickness that the survey can detect for $\phi = 0.25$.

**★★5.** From the synthetic Gassmann frame of the lesson
($K_{\text{dry}} = 1.0$, $\mu_{\text{dry}} = 3.0$, $K_{\text{min}} = 37\
\mathrm{GPa}$, $\phi = 0.30$), compute $K_{\text{sat}}$, $v_p$ and $v_s$ for
pores filled with air ($K = 10^{5}\ \mathrm{Pa}$, $\rho = 1.2\
\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$) and with water ($K = 2.2\ \mathrm{GPa}$, $\rho = 1000\
\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$). Explain in one sentence why $v_s$ falls.

**★★6.** A refraction survey finds a water table using
$v_1 = 1640\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ above and $v_2 = 2270\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
below, with a crossover distance of $37\ \mathrm{m}$. Compute the depth, and
then compute the depth error if $v_1$ were really $1720\
\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ (+4.9%) while the crossover distance were unchanged.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a two-method experiment that separates salinity from clay
content in a conductive aquifer. State the two measurements, the two forward
relations you would use, the assumption each makes, and how you would test the
result. A specification that could not be executed is a failed answer.

**★★★8.** The Ghyben-Herzberg relation assumes a steady, hydrostatic, sharp
interface. Construct a simple quantitative argument for how pumping a well at a
head drawdown $\delta h$ changes the interface position, and state the sign and
rough magnitude of the effect for a sea-water ratio. Identify one observation
that would show the sharp-interface model to be failing.
