# Exercises — 11-07-03 Case Study: Geothermal Field Development

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-case-study-geothermal-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Case Study: Geothermal Field Development](../03-case-study-geothermal.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** argument archetypes: no real field or resource is
used, and no site-specific number is quoted.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the heat in place for a reservoir of area $8\ \mathrm{km^{2}}$,
thickness $1.2\ \mathrm{km}$ and temperature $90\ \mathrm{K}$ above reference,
with $\rho c = 2.5\times10^{6}\ \mathrm{J\,m^{-3}\,K^{-1}}$. Compute the
recoverable heat at a recovery factor of $0.10$, in EJ.

**★2.** A well produces $30\ \mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$ with a wellhead temperature
drop of $100\ \mathrm{K}$, $c_p = 4200\ \mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ and a
conversion efficiency of $0.10$. Compute the electric power in MWe and the
number of wells needed for $10\ \mathrm{MWe}$.

**★3.** An MT conductor has $P(\text{conductor}\mid\text{productive}) = 0.8$ and
$P(\text{conductor}\mid\text{dry}) = 0.5$. With a prior of $0.2$, compute the
posterior probability that the conductor indicates a productive reservoir.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** With $b = 1.1$ and a forecast of 5000 events above $M = 0$, compute the
expected numbers of events with $M \ge 2$ and $M \ge 3$.

**★★5.** A reservoir has $3.0\ \mathrm{EJ}$ in place. Compute the recoverable
heat and the average electrical output over a 30-year life for recovery factors
of $0.05$, $0.10$ and $0.20$ with $\eta = 0.10$. State which parameter the
answer is most sensitive to.

**★★6.** A slim hole costs $C$ and a confirmed resource is worth $20C$. An MT
conductor has the likelihoods of ★3. Compute the expected value of drilling at
priors of $0.30$ and $0.10$, and state how the prior affects the decision.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a geothermal exploration programme for a frontier volcanic
region: specify the methods (MT, gravity, microseismicity, and any other), what
each measures, the false-positive mode of each, and the decision rule that turns
a resistivity structure into a slim-hole location. Quote no site-specific numbers
(use synthetic teaching values if you need numbers).

**★★★8.** A regulator asks for a traffic-light protocol. Write it, including the
magnitude thresholds, the action for each level, the monitoring capability assumed
and the expected number of stoppages for a stated sequence size. Then state what
the protocol does *not* control, and the one measurement that would most improve
it.
