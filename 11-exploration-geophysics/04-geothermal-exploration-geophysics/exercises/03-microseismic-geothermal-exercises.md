# Exercises — 11-04-03 Microseismic Monitoring of Geothermal Fields

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-microseismic-geothermal-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Microseismic Monitoring of Geothermal Fields](../03-microseismic-geothermal.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** An event has $M_0 = 1.5\times10^{12}$ N m. Compute $M_w$ and state
which constant in the relation is a convention.

**★2.** For $\Delta\sigma = 2$ MPa, compute the rupture radius implied by the
moment of ★1.

**★3.** With $\mu = 25$ GPa, compute the mean slip for the moment of ★1 and the
radius of ★2, and compare it with the slip of a 200 m rupture with the same
moment.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Using the lesson's detection table, state the detection radius for
$M_w = 0$ and for $M_w = 2$, and explain in terms of corner frequency why the
second is so much larger.

**★★5.** A catalogue contains 400 events above $M_c = 1.0$ with a mean magnitude
of 1.45. Compute the maximum-likelihood $b$-value and state whether it is
consistent with the tectonic convention $b = 1$, and what would make you doubt
the estimate.

**★★6.** With $k = 5$ mD, $\mu_w = 3\times10^{-4}$ Pa s, $\phi = 0.04$ and
$c_t = 10^{-9}\ \mathrm{Pa^{-1}}$, compute the hydraulic diffusivity and the
distance the pressure front reaches in 30 days. State how the answer changes if
the permeability is 50 mD.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a monitoring network whose magnitude of completeness is
$M_w = 0$ throughout a 1.5 km by 1.5 km by 1 km reservoir at 3 km depth, using
the lesson's detection model. State the sensor positions, the count, the assumed
noise floor, the location accuracy you expect, and the one thing your design
cannot measure. Justify with numbers.

**★★★8.** Design a study that measures the $b$-value bias produced by a
distance-dependent detection threshold, with the synthetic volume, the magnitude
distribution, the detection model and the fitting procedure specified. Predict
the sign and rough size of the bias at low $M_c$, and state the failure mode
that would invalidate the result.
