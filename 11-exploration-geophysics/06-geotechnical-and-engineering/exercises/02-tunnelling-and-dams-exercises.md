# Exercises — 11-06-02 Geophysics for Tunnels, Dams and Foundations

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-tunnelling-and-dams-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Geophysics for Tunnels, Dams and Foundations](../02-tunnelling-and-dams.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** For $v_1 = 1200\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and
$v_2 = 3500\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, compute $\theta_c$, $\cos\theta_c$ and
$\mathrm{d}z/\mathrm{d}t_i$. State the depth error for a $2\ \mathrm{ms}$ pick
error.

**★2.** A $4\ \mathrm{m}$ zone with $v = 1800\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ sits in a
$3500\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ host. Compute the first-arrival delay and compare it
with a $1\ \mathrm{ms}$ picking error.

**★3.** Two point current sources of $\pm20\ \mathrm{mA}$ sit at 8 m and 24 m
depth in rock of $\rho = 150\ \Omega\,\mathrm{m}$. Compute the surface potential
directly above the pair ($x = 0$). State the units.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Using $\kappa = 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-1}}$, $L = 12\ \mathrm{m}$,
$T_{\text{top}} = 9\ ^\circ$C and $T_{\text{bot}} = 21\ ^\circ$C, compute
$T(4\ \mathrm{m})$ for $\mathrm{Pe} = 0$, $2$ and $10$, and the departure from
the conductive profile.

**★★5.** A look-ahead system has $A_0 = 1$ (arbitrary) and $d_0 = 20\ \mathrm{m}$.
Compute the detection range for noise-to-signal ratios of $0.04$ and $0.12$, and
state the scaling law that connects them.

**★★6.** An SP profile peaks at $18\ \mathrm{mV}$ with a half-width of 15 m.
Using the lesson's geometry (a vertical dipole of the same half-width scaling),
estimate the shallower source depth and, for $\rho = 80\
\Omega\,\mathrm{m}$, the current.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a monitoring plan for a dam's internal-erosion risk using at
least three observables (SP, temperature, resistivity, and one of your choice).
For each, state what it measures, its resolution and detection limit, and how you
would distinguish a real change from a seasonal or operational one. State the
false-negative mode.

**★★★8.** A tunnel face survey detects a 2 m low-velocity zone 20 m ahead. Write
the decision rule that converts this into an action (probe drill, grout, continue)
using expected cost, and state explicitly what the survey has *not* established.
Include the arithmetic of the probability of water inflow that would justify a
probe hole.
