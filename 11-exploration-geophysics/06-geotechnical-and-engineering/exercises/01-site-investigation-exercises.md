# Exercises — 11-06-01 Site Investigation: Objectives and Method Selection

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-site-investigation-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Site Investigation](../01-site-investigation.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Convert $v_s = 250\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ at
$\rho = 1800\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ into $G_{\max}$ and, with $\nu = 0.30$, into
$E$. State the units of each step.

**★2.** A profile is $200\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ to 5 m over
$500\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ from 5 to 30 m. Compute $v_{s30}$, showing the travel
time through each interval.

**★3.** Compute the peak gravity anomaly of a $1.5\ \mathrm{m}$ radius air-filled
void at $6\ \mathrm{m}$ depth in rock of density $2600\
\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, in $\mu\mathrm{Gal}$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** For the profile of ★2, compute the elastic settlement of a flexible
circular footing with $R = 2.0\ \mathrm{m}$ and $q = 150\ \mathrm{kPa}$ using
$\nu = 0.30$ and an operational modulus equal to $0.35\,E_{\max}$. Then compute
the change if $v_s$ is $15\%$ lower everywhere.

**★★5.** A $5\ \mu\mathrm{Gal}$ microgravity survey must find a void at
$12\ \mathrm{m}$ depth. What void radius is required in $2500\
\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ rock? State the assumption about the shape.

**★★6.** A site has a prior probability $0.20$ of a buried obstruction. The
survey has $\mathrm{TPR} = 0.9$ and $\mathrm{FPR} = 0.25$; the survey costs $c$,
the protective measure $8c$ and a hit $40c$. Compute the value of information in
units of $c$ and state whether to survey.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a method suite for a hospital site on a karstic limestone where
the design questions are (i) cavity detection to $15\ \mathrm{m}$, (ii) bedrock
depth, and (iii) a stiffness profile for a settlement calculation. For each
question state the method, the resolution or detection number you expect from the
lesson's relations, and the failure mode that would invalidate the answer.

**★★★8.** The lesson's value-of-information model uses fixed $\mathrm{TPR}$ and
$\mathrm{FPR}$. Explain how tuning the anomaly threshold changes both, derive the
trade-off (ROC) qualitatively, and state how a decision-maker should choose the
threshold. Describe one way an investigator could accidentally overstate the VOI.
