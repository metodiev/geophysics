# Exercises — 11-03-02 Seismic Stratigraphy and Sequence Interpretation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-seismic-stratigraphy-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Seismic Stratigraphy and Sequence Interpretation](../02-seismic-stratigraphy.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A thin bed of thickness $h = 12\ \mathrm{m}$ has velocity
$3.0\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. Compute the two-way separation $\Delta t$ and the
magnitude of the interference factor $2|\sin(\pi f\Delta t)|$ at 15, 30 and
60 Hz.

**★2.** Using the coefficients measured in the demonstration, compute the tuning
thickness and the thickness at which the read-off becomes accurate to 10%, for a
25 Hz wavelet and $v = 3.0\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. Compare both with $\lambda/4$.

**★3.** A 6 m bed is imaged with a 25 Hz wavelet in 3.0 km/s rock. Compute its
true two-way time and predict the read-off time from the wavelet's own
peak-to-trough separation (use the peak-to-trough time $= 1/(2f_0)$ as an order
of magnitude).

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A composite amplitude of 0.200 (dimensionless) is measured on a
wedge whose reflection coefficients are $\pm R = \pm0.10$, imaged with a 30 Hz
wavelet in 3.0 km/s rock. State the thickness range consistent with the
demonstration's curve and explain what quantity the amplitude actually
determines in that regime.

**★★5.** A shelf has subsidence $50\ \mathrm{m\,Myr^{-1}}$, a eustatic cycle of
amplitude 30 m and period 0.4 Myr, and a sediment supply of
$80\ \mathrm{m\,Myr^{-1}}$. Compute the accommodation rate as a function of
time, its range, and the tract at $t = 0.1$ Myr. State whether forced
regression occurs.

**★★6.** Noise is 5% of the thick-bed composite amplitude. A 2 m bed and a 20 m
bed are both present. Using the demonstration's numbers, decide for each whether
it is (a) detectable and (b) resolvable, and justify the two answers separately.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a measurement that determines the tuning coefficient
(expressed as a multiple of $v/f_0$) for three different wavelets, and state the
metric you would use for "thickness is readable". Predict the ordering of the
coefficients from the wavelets' bandwidths, and state the experiment's failure
mode. A specification that could not be executed is a failed answer.

**★★★8.** Derive the interference factor $2|\sin(\pi f \Delta t)|$ from the
two-reflector model, show that a narrow-band wavelet centred on $f_0$ has its
composite maximum at $\Delta t = 1/(2f_0)$ (hence $h = \lambda/4$), and explain
why a broadband Ricker measures $0.195\,v/f_0$ instead. State which number you
would use in a report, and why.
