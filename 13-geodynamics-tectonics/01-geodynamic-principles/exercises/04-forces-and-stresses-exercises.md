# Exercises — 13-01-04 Driving Forces, Stress Fields and Intraplate Stress

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-forces-and-stresses-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 Driving Forces](../04-forces-and-stresses.md). All models are **SYNTHETIC**;
$g = 9.81\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and
$\rho_m = 3300\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$,
$\alpha = 3\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$, $T_m = 1300$ K,
$\kappa = 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-1}}$, $\rho_w = 1030\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$
where stated.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** The lesson measures a thermal subsidence of 3593.8 m at 100 Myr. Compute
the subsidence at 25 Myr and at 400 Myr, and state the scaling with age.

**★2.** The lesson measures a ridge push of
$4.11\times10^{12}\ \mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$ at 100 Myr. Compute it at 25 Myr and at
50 Myr, stating the scaling with age and why it differs from the subsidence
scaling.

**★3.** State the SI unit of GPE per unit area and show by dimensional analysis
that it equals a force per unit length.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A SYNTHETIC slab is 80 km thick and 500 km long down-dip, with the
cooling-plate thermal structure of the lesson at 100 Myr. Compute the density
anomaly integral across the slab, the mean anomaly, and slab pull per unit trench
length. Compare with the 100 km slab's $42.04\ \mathrm{TN\,m^{-1}}$ and state
whether slab pull is closer to linear or to saturating in slab thickness.

**★★5.** Using the $F_{SP}$ from ★★4 and the lesson's ridge push, compute the net
driving force and the implied plate speed for
$\eta = 5\times10^{20}\ \mathrm{Pa\,s}$, $d = 200$ km and
$L_p = 5000$ km. State the factor by which the speed changed and why it is exactly
that factor.

**★★6.** Derive the closed form
$\Delta\mathrm{GPE} = \tfrac{g}{2}(h_2^{2}-h_1^{2})(\rho_m-\rho_c)\rho_c/\rho_m$
for two Airy-compensated columns with crustal thicknesses $h_1$ and $h_2$. State
the two assumptions the derivation needs.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Build an uncertainty budget for the force inventory. Take
$\pm10\%$ on $\Delta\rho$, $\pm25\%$ on the slab length, $\pm50\%$ on
$\eta$ and $\pm20\%$ on the drag-layer thickness $d$, all independent. State which
of these dominates the uncertainty in the implied plate speed, and which would
dominate if the question were the *relative* speeds of two plates with different
slab lengths.

**★★★8.** The lesson computes an *upper bound* of 109 MPa on the mean deviatoric
stress in a 70 km thick plateau. Write a 150–250 word critique of a paper that
reports "the intraplate stress is 109 MPa". Your critique must identify the
specific step in the derivation that turns a bound into an estimate, and state
what additional data would make the number defensible.

**★★★9.** Design a test of whether ridge push or slab pull dominates the motion of
a specific plate. State the observable, the model, the expected signature of each
hypothesis, and the confound that would make the test inconclusive.
