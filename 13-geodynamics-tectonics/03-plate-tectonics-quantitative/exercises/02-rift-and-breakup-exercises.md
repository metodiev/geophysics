# Exercises — 13-03-02 Rifting, Break-Up and Passive Margin Formation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](02-rift-and-breakup-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Rifting, Break-Up and Passive Margin Formation](../02-rift-and-breakup.md).
All models and data in this module are **SYNTHETIC**.

Unless stated otherwise use: $a = 125\ \mathrm{km}$, $t_{c0} = 35\ \mathrm{km}$,
$T_m = 1300\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $\alpha = 3\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$,
$\rho_m = 3330$, $\rho_c = 2800$, $\rho_w = 1030\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$,
$\kappa = 31.558\ \mathrm{km^{2}\,Myr^{-1}}$, $A = 8.065\ \mathrm{km}$,
$B = 1.829\ \mathrm{km}$.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A lithospheric column with $t_{c0} = 32\ \mathrm{km}$ and
$a = 110\ \mathrm{km}$ is stretched uniformly by $\beta = 1.6$. State the new
crustal and lithospheric thicknesses, the finite extension in per cent, and the
natural strain.

**★2.** For the same column ($t_{c0} = 32\ \mathrm{km}$, $a = 110\ \mathrm{km}$)
compute $A$ and $B$ and hence $S(0)$ for $\beta = 1.6$. Show the arithmetic.

**★3.** Compute $\tau = a^{2}/(\pi^{2}\kappa)$ for $a = 110\ \mathrm{km}$ and
for $a = 200\ \mathrm{km}$, and state in words what the ratio means.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A rift reaches $\beta = 2.5$ in $12\ \mathrm{Myr}$. Compute the
natural strain, the strain rate in $\mathrm{Myr^{-1}}$ and in nanostrain per
year, and the equatorial velocity gradient across a $100\ \mathrm{km}$ rift
section.

**★★5.** Using $S(\infty) = A(1-1/\beta) + B_{\text{ref}} - B_{\text{str}}$
with the terms evaluated as in the lesson, compute the total thermal subsidence
$S(\infty) - S(0)$ for $\beta = 1.5$ and for $\beta = 1.25$, and show that the
thermal part does **not** scale linearly with the crustal part.

**★★6.** A margin's post-rift subsidence is fitted from $t = 0$ with a single
exponential and yields $\tau = 43\ \mathrm{Myr}$. Using the demonstration's
measured bias, estimate the $\tau$ that would have been obtained by fitting only
after $25\ \mathrm{Myr}$, and state which one should be compared with
$a^{2}/(\pi^{2}\kappa)$.

**★★7.** The water-loaded initial subsidence is measured as
$3.4 \pm 0.3\ \mathrm{km}$. Convert that to a $\beta$ range and state the
dominant contribution to the uncertainty if the densities are known to $1\%$.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★8.** Consider **crust-only thinning**: the crust is thinned by $\beta_f = 1.5$
while the mantle lithosphere keeps its pre-rift geometry and temperature
structure. Derive $S(0)$ from isostasy, compare it with the uniform model at the
same $\beta$, and explain why the difference is exactly a factor
$A/(A-B)$ in this limit. Then state what the **post-rift** thermal subsidence
does, and which observation would distinguish this model from uniform
stretching.

**★★★9.** Design a SYNTHETIC experiment to determine whether the post-rift
subsidence of a margin is better explained by (i) one stretching event with
$\beta$ and $\tau$ free, or (ii) two stretching events $\beta_1$ then $\beta_2$
separated by $\Delta t$. Specify the data, the noise model, the metric, and the
circumstances under which the two models become indistinguishable. You do not
need to run it, but a specification that could not be executed is a failed
answer.
