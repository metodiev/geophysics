# Exercises — 13-02-04 Subduction Dynamics, Slab Geometry and Slab Pull

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-subduction-dynamics-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 Subduction Dynamics](../04-subduction-dynamics.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC**; $g = 9.81\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$,
$E = 70\ \mathrm{GPa}$, $\nu = 0.25$,
$\kappa = 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^{2}\,s^{-1}}$.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A slab 60 km thick and 500 km long down-dip has a mean density anomaly of
$50\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Compute slab pull per unit trench length in
$\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$ and TN m$^{-1}$.

**★2.** For the slab of ★1 in a mantle of $\eta = 5\times10^{20}\ \mathrm{Pa\,s}$,
compute the sinking speed in $\mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ and the time to descend
660 km.

**★3.** Compute the flexural rigidity for $T_e = 20$ km and the bending length
$l_b = (2D/q)^{1/3}$ with $q = \Delta\rho\,g\,h$ from ★1. State the unit of
$l_b$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** For the slab of ★1, compute the thickness of the elastic core that can
bend to a radius of 250 km if the yield strength is 500 MPa. Compare with the slab
thickness and state what the comparison implies for any model that treats the slab
as a thick elastic plate.

**★★5.** Combine the slab pull of ★1 with the ridge push of the module
($4.11\ \mathrm{TN\,m^{-1}}$) and compute the implied plate speed for
$\eta = 10^{21}\ \mathrm{Pa\,s}$, a drag-layer thickness of 200 km and a plate
length of 5000 km. State which of the three input quantities the answer is most
sensitive to.

**★★6.** Compute the thermal equilibration time of the slab of ★1,
$t_{\text{eq}} = (h/2)^{2}/\kappa$, and the depth at which the anomaly would be
erased if the slab sank at 70 mm yr$^{-1}$. State whether such a slab should be
seismically detectable at 1500 km depth.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a test of whether a slab stagnates at the 660 km discontinuity or
passes through it. State the data, the forward model, the diagnostic, the expected
signature of each hypothesis, and the trade-off between a viscosity increase at
660 km and a phase-change buoyancy that would mimic it.

**★★★8.** For a slab segment 500 km long, compute the dip change per Myr for
trench rollback rates of 10, 20 and 30 mm yr$^{-1}$. State why the convergence
rate does not appear, and describe the observation that would break the
degeneracy between rollback-driven steepening and buoyancy-driven steepening.
