# Exercises — 10-02-01 Survey Design

Attempt before the [solutions](./01-gpr-survey-design-solutions.md). Lesson:
[01 Survey Design](../01-gpr-survey-design.md). All models **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** For $f = 100\ \mathrm{MHz}$ and $\varepsilon_r = 16$, compute $\lambda$
and the trace spacing required to record dips up to $90^\circ$ and up to
$30^\circ$.

**★2.** Compute the time window and sample count for a deepest target at
$4.50\ \mathrm{m}$ in $v = 0.07495\ \mathrm{m\,ns^{-1}}$ with a $15\
\mathrm{ns}$ guard at $0.05\ \mathrm{ns}$ sampling.

**★3.** Compute the Fresnel diameter at $2.0\ \mathrm{m}$ for
$\lambda = 0.4996\ \mathrm{m}$ and state the line spacing above which detection
probability falls below one.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A $25\ \mathrm{m} \times 15\ \mathrm{m}$ grid at $0.05\ \mathrm{m}$
trace spacing and $0.30\ \mathrm{m}$ line spacing: compute lines, traces, total
traces and profiled distance.

**★★5.** For a target at $2.0\ \mathrm{m}$ in $v = 0.075\ \mathrm{m\,ns^{-1}}$,
compute the offset required for a $8\ \mathrm{ns}$ moveout, and the velocity error
from a $0.25\ \mathrm{ns}$ pick error at that offset.

**★★6.** A cross-hole panel has $15$ sources and $25$ receivers over $8\
\mathrm{m}$ in boreholes $4\ \mathrm{m}$ apart. Estimate the ray count and state,
without computing, which regions will be poorly covered and why.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a complete acquisition for a $50\ \mathrm{m} \times 12\
\mathrm{m}$ site, target depth $3\ \mathrm{m}$, dips up to $40^\circ$,
$\varepsilon_r = 12$, wanting the velocity to $3\%$. Give frequency, trace
spacing, line spacing, offsets, time window and the resulting data volume, and
state each assumption.

**★★★8.** A grid is designed with line spacing equal to the Fresnel diameter at
$1.5\ \mathrm{m}$. Argue, with numbers, whether that spacing would satisfy a
requirement to *resolve* two $0.5\ \mathrm{m}$-wide targets $0.4\ \mathrm{m}$
apart, and state the spacing you would recommend instead.
