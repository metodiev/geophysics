# Exercises — 10-02-04 3-D GPR Imaging and Time Slices

Attempt before the [solutions](./04-gpr-3d-imaging-solutions.md). Lesson:
[04 3-D Imaging](../04-gpr-3d-imaging.md). All models **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A cube covers $6\ \mathrm{m} \times 4\ \mathrm{m}$ with lines every
$0.20\ \mathrm{m}$ and traces every $0.05\ \mathrm{m}$, $2000$ samples at
$0.05\ \mathrm{ns}$. Compute lines, traces and data volume at two bytes per
sample.

**★2.** Compute the shift rate $\mathrm{d}y/\mathrm{d}t$ of the time slice of a
plane dipping $30^\circ$ in $v = 0.075\ \mathrm{m\,ns^{-1}}$.

**★3.** For $\lambda = 0.4328\ \mathrm{m}$ and $\Delta y = 0.30\ \mathrm{m}$,
compute the maximum unaliased dip.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A band moves $0.30\ \mathrm{m}$ between slices $2.0\ \mathrm{ns}$ apart
in $v = 0.09993\ \mathrm{m\,ns^{-1}}$. Compute the dip.

**★★5.** Using the relation for velocity error, compute the depth-converted
apparent dip of a $15^\circ$ interface if the conversion velocity is $8\%$ too
high, and the lateral position error at $2.0\ \mathrm{m}$ depth for a plane with
$d_0 = 1.0\ \mathrm{m}$.

**★★6.** A time slice at $30\ \mathrm{ns}$ has a maximum of $0.90$ at
$(x, y) = (1.50, 2.00)\ \mathrm{m}$ on a $0.10\ \mathrm{m}$ grid. State the
position you would report and why.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** A $25^\circ$ plane is imaged with lines every $0.40\ \mathrm{m}$ in a
medium where $\lambda$ varies from $0.30$ to $0.60\ \mathrm{m}$ along the line
because the moisture changes. Compute the apparent dip in the two zones and state
the interpretive consequence.

**★★★8.** Argue, with the numbers of the lesson, whether a $4\ \mathrm{m}
\times 4\ \mathrm{m}$ cube with $0.25\ \mathrm{m}$ line spacing and $0.05\
\mathrm{m}$ trace spacing can resolve two targets $0.3\ \mathrm{m}$ apart
laterally at $1.5\ \mathrm{m}$ depth, and state which measurement you would make
to test the claim.
