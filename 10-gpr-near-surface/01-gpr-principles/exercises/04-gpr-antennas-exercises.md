# Exercises — 10-01-04 Antennas: Frequency, Shielding, Polarisation and Offsets

Attempt before the [solutions](./04-gpr-antennas-solutions.md). Lesson:
[04 Antennas](../04-gpr-antennas.md). All models **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the half-wave dipole length for $250\ \mathrm{MHz}$ in air and
in a medium with $\varepsilon_r = 9$.

**★2.** Compute the critical angle for $\varepsilon_r = 6.25$ and the footprint
radius at $1.5\ \mathrm{m}$ depth.

**★3.** Antennas $0.50\ \mathrm{m}$ apart over ground with $v = 0.075\
\mathrm{m\,ns^{-1}}$: compute the air-wave and ground-wave arrival times and the
blind depth.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A target responds with $40\%$ of its $0^\circ$ amplitude. Compute the
two possible angles and the power factor in each case.

**★★5.** Compute the fraction of a dipole's radiated power inside a $\pm30^\circ$
cone and compare it with the $\pm45^\circ$ value.

**★★6.** For a target at $0.40\ \mathrm{m}$ depth in $v = 0.09993\
\mathrm{m\,ns^{-1}}$, compute the two-way time at zero offset and at
$x = 0.40\ \mathrm{m}$, and the depth error if the offset is ignored.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** A $0.150\ \mathrm{m}$ dipole is designed for air. State its resonance
frequency in three media and argue, with numbers, why a survey cannot simply
relabel the same antenna as a different frequency band when the ground moisture
changes.

**★★★8.** Using the footprint radius $d/\sqrt{\varepsilon_r-1}$ and the Fresnel
diameter of [05 Resolution Limits](../05-gpr-resolution.md), compare the two
lengths at $1\ \mathrm{m}$ depth for $\varepsilon_r = 9$, $f = 200\
\mathrm{MHz}$, and state which one limits detection and which limits resolution.
