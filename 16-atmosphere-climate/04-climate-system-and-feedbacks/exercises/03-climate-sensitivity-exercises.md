# Exercises — 16-04-03 Climate Sensitivity: Definitions and Estimation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-climate-sensitivity-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Climate Sensitivity](../03-climate-sensitivity.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values. Use $F_{2\times} = 3.7\
\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$ throughout.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute ECS for $|\lambda| = 4.0\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}K^{-1}}$, and TCR
from the approximation $F_{2\times}/(|\lambda|+\kappa)$ with
$\kappa = 0.76\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}K^{-1}}$. Give the ratio TCR/ECS.

**★2.** Compute the doubling time for a 0.5% yr⁻¹ CO₂ increase and for a
2% yr⁻¹ increase. State, with a reason, which pathway gives the larger TCR.

**★3.** For the model of Section 2 ($|\lambda| = 3.5$, $\gamma = 0.7$,
$C_m = 2.04\times10^8$, $C_d = 1.37\times10^{10}$ J m⁻² K⁻¹), state whether
doubling ECS would double TCR, and why or why not.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** From $\text{TCR} = F_{2\times}/(|\lambda|+\kappa)$ with
$|\lambda| = 3.5$, compute TCR for $\kappa \in \{0.4, 0.8, 1.4\}$ and the
limiting value as $\kappa\to0$. State what the limit means physically.

**★★5.** Assume the total feedback is uniform on $[-3.5, -2.0]$
W m⁻² K⁻¹. Compute the median ECS, the 5–95% range, and the ratio of the upper
half-width of that range to the lower one. Comment on what the ratio says about
reporting a symmetric uncertainty.

**★★6.** A Gregory regression over 150 years returns a slope of
$-4.5\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}K^{-1}}$ and an intercept of $3.88\
\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$.
**(a)** Compute the implied ECS.
**(b)** The true forcing is 3.7 W m⁻². State whether the intercept error is
consistent with a drifting feedback that strengthens with warming, and why.
**(c)** State one diagnostic from the lesson that would help decide.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** A study reports ECS from a Gregory regression over 150 years with
$R^2 = 0.998$. Construct the argument that this does not establish the value,
using the lesson's window table as evidence. Then state the diagnostic you would
request and what pattern in it would indicate a drift-induced bias.

**★★★8.** Design a synthetic experiment to determine how many years of a 1%
yr⁻¹ ramp are needed before a Gregory regression's implied ECS is within 10% of
the model's true equilibrium, using the drifting-feedback model of Section 3.
State the metric, the quantity you would plot against what abscissa, and the
result that would show the estimate is *stable* rather than merely accurate at
one window.
