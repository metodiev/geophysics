# Exercises — 16-06-04 Pleistocene Glacial Cycles and Orbital Forcing

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-pleistocene-cycles-solutions.md). Lesson:
[04 Pleistocene Glacial Cycles and Orbital Forcing](../04-pleistocene-cycles.md).
All models and data are **SYNTHETIC**; the periods are the standard astronomical
ones, the amplitudes are not. The demonstration is
[`../code/pleistocene_cycles_demo.py`](../code/pleistocene_cycles_demo.py).

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** The demonstration reports forcing variance shares of $0.2723$
(obliquity), $0.6831$ (precession) and $0.0026$ (eccentricity). Sum them and
state where the remainder lies.

**★2.** The response shares in the same bands are $0.2042$, $0.1536$ and
$0.3073$. Compute the power amplification required in the eccentricity band
relative to the forcing, and the corresponding amplitude factor.

**★3.** The growth term is $\mathrm dV/\mathrm dt = g(1-V)$ with $g = 1/60\
\mathrm{ka^{-1}}$. Compute the e-folding time and the time to grow from $V = 0$
to $V = 0.633$ with melting switched off.

## ★★ Applied

**★4.** The demonstration's record is $2400\ \mathrm{ka}$ long and reports a
dominant period of $100.0\ \mathrm{ka}$. Compute the frequency resolution and the
implied uncertainty in the period, as a percentage.

**★5.** Compute the response-to-forcing variance ratio in the obliquity,
precession and eccentricity bands from the shares in ★1 and ★2. State which band
shows amplification and which shows attenuation.

**★6.** The threshold sensitivity table reports a long-period share of $0.3598$
at threshold $0.60$ and $0.5066$ at $0.80$, with mean volumes $0.2182$ and
$0.3103$. Compute the change in each per unit of threshold and interpret the
signs.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design an observational or modelling test that would distinguish a
threshold-driven response from an internally oscillating ice sheet as the cause
of the observed glacial cycles. State the diagnostic, the expected signature of
each mechanism, and one ambiguity that remains.

**★★★8.** Write a short critique of the claim "the demonstration shows that
orbital forcing explains the 100-kyr cycles". State what the demonstration
actually establishes and what would be required to support the claim.
