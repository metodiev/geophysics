# Exercises — 16-09-02 Geodesy for Sea Level, Ice Mass and Water Storage

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-geodesy-climate-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Geodesy for Sea Level, Ice Mass and Water Storage](../02-geodesy-climate.md).
All models and numbers are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values in mm/yr.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A budget has components total $= 3.50$, mass $= 2.10$ and steric
$= 1.30\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$, with standard errors $0.40$, $0.20$ and
$0.30\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$. Compute the closure residual, its $1\sigma$
uncertainty, and the ratio residual/$\sigma$.

**★2.** Express a $3.3\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ sea-level trend as the total change
over $30$ years in millimetres, and do the same for a gauge trend of
$4.5\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$.

**★3.** A mass term of $1.70\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ receives a GIA correction of
$+0.60\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$. Compute the corrected value and the fractional
change the correction produces.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A gauge sits where the land subsides at $-1.50\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$,
and its trend is quoted as $+4.50\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ with a land-motion
uncertainty of $\pm0.30\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$. Compute the absolute trend and its
uncertainty, and state the two contributions to that uncertainty.

**★★5.** In the demonstration the budget residual uncertainty including the GIA
term is $0.867\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ and the GIA uncertainty alone is
$0.25\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$. Compute the fraction of the total variance the GIA
term contributes, and state what happens to that fraction if the GIA uncertainty
were halved.

**★★6.** The demonstration's trend standard errors are $1.734\
\mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ at $n = 10$ years and $0.434\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ at
$n = 30$. Compute the observed ratio of the two and compare it with the
$n^{-3/2}$ prediction. State how much longer a record you would need to keep the
same trend detectability if the noise standard deviation doubled.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** A gravity-based study reports that a coastal region loses mass at
$2\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ water equivalent, attributing $1.2$ to ice and $0.8$ to
groundwater. Design the validation you would require before accepting the
partition, naming the two corrections most likely to change it and the specific
diagnostic for each.

**★★★8.** Design a synthetic experiment to measure how large a GIA uncertainty
can be tolerated before a two-term budget cannot distinguish (a) all ice loss from
(b) a mix of ice loss and groundwater depletion. Specify the generator, the
estimator, the decision rule, and the result that defines the tolerance.
