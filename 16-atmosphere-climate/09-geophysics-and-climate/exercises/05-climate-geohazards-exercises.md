# Exercises — 16-09-05 Climate-Geohazard Interactions

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./05-climate-geohazards-solutions.md). The lesson is
[05 Climate-Geohazard Interactions](../05-climate-geohazards.md). All models and
numbers are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** For $\gamma = 18.0\ \mathrm{kN\,m^{-3}}$, $c' = 5.0\ \mathrm{kPa}$,
$\phi' = 30^\circ$, $\beta = 22^\circ$ and $z = 3.0\ \mathrm{m}$, compute the
factor of safety at $r_u = 0.3$ and state whether the slope is stable.

**★2.** A soil column has an excess-ice volume fraction of $0.25$ and its active
layer deepens from $1.2$ to $1.9\ \mathrm{m}$. Compute the settlement.

**★3.** Two exceedance events have $\Pr(A) = 0.05$, $\Pr(B) = 0.08$ and
$\Pr(A \cap B) = 0.01$. Compute $\Pr(A \cup B)$ and state which quantity a
compound-flood impact would use if the impact requires both drivers.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A storm surge is lognormal with median $1.20\ \mathrm{m}$ and log-scale
$0.35$. Compute the exceedance probability of a $1.80\ \mathrm{m}$ threshold and
of an effective $1.50\ \mathrm{m}$ threshold (after a $0.30\ \mathrm{m}$
sea-level rise), and give the ratio.

**★★5.** Thaw settlement is $\Delta z = f_{\text{ice}}(h_{\text{warm}} -
h_{\text{now}})$ with $h_{\text{warm}} - h_{\text{now}} = 0.8\ \mathrm{m}$ and
$f_{\text{ice}} = 0.30 \pm 0.10$. Compute the settlement range and state which
input dominates the uncertainty.

**★★6.** For a cohesionless soil, $F = 1$ when $\tan\beta = (1-r_u)\tan\phi'$.
For $\beta = 25^\circ$ and $\phi' = 32^\circ$, compute the critical $r_u$, and
state what an increase of pore pressure above it would do.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study that would quantify how much of the change in compound
coastal flood frequency over a chosen period is attributable to sea-level rise
versus to a change in the dependence (correlation) between surge and river stage.
Specify the data, the model, the metric and the confounder you must control.

**★★★8.** Design a monitoring and modelling programme that would distinguish a
slope failure driven by permafrost thaw from one driven by increased rainfall,
for the same slope. Specify the observables, the thresholds, and the pattern of
results that would leave the two mechanisms indistinguishable.
