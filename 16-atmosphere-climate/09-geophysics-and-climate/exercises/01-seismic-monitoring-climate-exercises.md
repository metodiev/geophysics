# Exercises — 16-09-01 Seismic and Geodetic Monitoring of Cryosphere Change

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-seismic-monitoring-climate-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Seismic and Geodetic Monitoring of Cryosphere Change](../01-seismic-monitoring-climate.md).
All models and numbers are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** An STA/LTA detector has an STA of $3.0$ and an LTA of $1.2$ at a
candidate time. Compute the ratio and say whether a threshold of $2.5$ is
exceeded.

**★2.** A medium's wave speed falls by $0.2\%$. State the sign and size of the
relative coda stretch $\varepsilon$, and compute the change in a $10\ \mathrm{s}$
travel time.

**★3.** The demonstration finds $36$ of $40$ events at threshold $8.0$. Compute
the miss rate and state what the threshold would have to be to find all $40$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** At threshold $2.0$ the detector fires $15$ times on a $600\ \mathrm{s}$
noise-only record. Convert this to false alarms per hour, and estimate the number
of false alarms in a one-year continuous deployment.

**★★5.** At a noise level of $0.20$ the standard deviation of the recovered stretch
is $1.1\times10^{-4}$. Compute the $2\sigma$ detection threshold and compare it
with a $0.05\%$ stretch. State whether the signal is resolvable at that noise
level.

**★★6.** A study reports a relative velocity change of $+0.30\%$ with a noise
floor of $\pm0.04\%$. Convert this to $\mathrm{d}v/v$ with its uncertainty, and
write the one-sentence result as it should appear in an abstract.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a synthetic study to measure how well an STA/LTA detector
recovers a *seasonal* cycle in icequake rate, given that the noise level also
varies seasonally. Specify the generator, the estimator, the metric, and the
result that would show the rate cycle is an artefact of the noise cycle.

**★★★8.** Design a joint seismic–geodetic experiment that would distinguish a
firn-thinning signal from a meltwater-content signal in a measured velocity
change. Specify the observables, the forward relations you would need, and the
pattern of results that would leave the two mechanisms indistinguishable.
