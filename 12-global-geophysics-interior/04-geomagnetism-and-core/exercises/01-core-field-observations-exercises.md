# Exercises — 12-04-01 Observations of the Core Field and Secular Variation

Attempt before the [solutions](./01-core-field-observations-solutions.md). Lesson:
[01 Observations of the Core Field and Secular Variation](../01-core-field-observations.md).
All models **SYNTHETIC**; the coefficients and sites below are invented teaching
inputs.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A synthetic model has $g_1^0 = -30\,000\ \mathrm{nT}$,
$g_1^1 = 0$, $h_1^1 = +5\,000\ \mathrm{nT}$. Compute the geocentric dipole
moment $M$ and the tilt of the dipole axis from the rotation axis.

**★2.** For a pure axial dipole with $g_1^0 = -30\,000\ \mathrm{nT}$, compute
$B_r$ and $B_\theta$ at colatitudes 45° and 90°, and state the inclination at
each. Then state what the answer at 90° would become if the dipole were tilted
by 9.5°.

**★3.** Compute the free-decay time $R^2/(\pi^2\eta)$ of the dipole mode for
$\sigma = 5\times10^{5}\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$ and $R = 3.48\times10^6\ \mathrm{m}$,
and state how the result scales with $\sigma$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Derive the free-decay eigenvalue condition
$(2l+1)j_l(x) - x j_{l+1}(x) = 0$ from the matching of an interior solution
$j_l(kr)$ to a decaying exterior potential, and compute the decay time of the
$l = 2$ fundamental using the root $x = 4.49341$.

**★★5.** Compute the magnetic Reynolds number for a flow of
$1\ \mathrm{mm\,s^{-1}}$ with $\sigma = 10^6\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$, and repeat for
$\sigma = 10^5\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$. State the conclusion in one sentence for
each case and say which number — $Rm$ or the decay time — is more sensitive to
the conductivity.

**★★6.** A truncated model to degree 6 has an rms residual of 2 899 nT against a
full model whose rms field is $42\,000\ \mathrm{nT}$. Express the residual as a
fraction, and state precisely what the fraction is a fraction *of*. Then say why
the fraction would change if the omitted degrees were crustal rather than core
field.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Derive the inclination at the magnetic equator as a function of the
dipole tilt for a dipole whose equatorial coefficient $h_1^1$ is non-zero and
$g_1^1 = 0$. Evaluate it for a tilt of 9.5° and state whether a single
equatorial site could measure the tilt.

**★★★8.** Design an experiment to measure the bias in a recovered westward-drift
rate caused by the non-advecting axial part of the field. Specify the synthetic
data you would generate, the estimator you would use, and the quantity you would
report as the bias.

## Notes on answers

Answers must distinguish a measured vector from a fitted coefficient, and a
derived quantity from a measured one. A decay time quoted without a conductivity
is not an answer.
