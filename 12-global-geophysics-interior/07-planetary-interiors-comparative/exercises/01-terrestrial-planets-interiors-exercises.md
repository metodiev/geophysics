# Exercises — 12-07-01 Interiors of Mars, Venus, Mercury and the Moon

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-terrestrial-planets-interiors-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Interiors of Mars, Venus, Mercury and the Moon](../01-terrestrial-planets-interiors.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values; none of them is a real body.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A uniform spherical body has $\rho = 3500\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and
$R = 2.5\times10^{6}$ m. Compute its mass, its central pressure from
$P_{c} = \tfrac23\pi G\rho^{2}R^{2}$, and the dimensionless
$\Pi = P_{c}R^{4}/(GM^{2})$. Compare $\Pi$ with $3/(8\pi)$.

**★2.** Two bodies have the same interior structure. Body 2 has twice the mass
and 1.5 times the radius of body 1. Using $P_{c}\propto GM^{2}/R^{4}$, compute
the ratio of their central pressures and state which is more compressed if they
have the same bulk modulus.

**★3.** Compute the surface-to-volume ratio $3/R$ for $R = 1.8\times10^{6}$ m and
for $R = 6.4\times10^{6}$ m, and give their ratio. State in one sentence what
this says about cooling.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** For the adopted $C/MR^{2} = 0.3350$, the lesson's ridge gives
$\alpha = 9.0515$ at $x_{\text{core}} = 0.30$ for $M = 4.0\times10^{23}$ kg and
$R = 3.0\times10^{6}$ m. Compute the mean density, the mantle density and the
core density, and comment on the plausibility of the core density.

**★★5.** The figure solver returns $J_{2}/q = 0.4979$ for a homogeneous body at
$q = 4\times10^{-3}$. Compute $k_{f} = 3J_{2}/q$ and compare it with the exact
small-rotation value $1.5$. State why the two differ and whether the difference
is a solver error.

**★★6.** A body has $\Pi = 0.1888$. Compute its central pressure for
$M = 8.0\times10^{23}$ kg and $R = 4.5\times10^{6}$ m. Then compute what the
central pressure would be if the *same* body were uniform
($\Pi = 3/(8\pi)$), and state what physical assumption the difference
represents.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** You are asked how precisely $C/MR^{2}$ and $k_{f} = 3J_{2}/q$ must be
measured to distinguish a two-layer interior from a three-layer one. Using the
spread of $k_{f}$ along the two-layer ridge (from $0.9922$ to $0.9607$ over
$x = 0.20$ to $0.60$), specify the required precisions and state the assumption
that would remain even after both measurements.

**★★★8.** Derive the exact uniform-sphere relation
$P_{c} = \tfrac{3}{8\pi}GM^{2}/R^{4}$ from hydrostatic equilibrium, showing the
integration. Then state what changes when the density is not uniform: does
$P_{c}R^{4}/(GM^{2})$ rise or fall with central condensation, and why?
