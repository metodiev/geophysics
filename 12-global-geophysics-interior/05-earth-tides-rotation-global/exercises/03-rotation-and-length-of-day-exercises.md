# Exercises — 12-05-03 Rotation Variations, LOD and Core-Mantle Coupling

Attempt before the
[solutions](./03-rotation-and-length-of-day-solutions.md). Lesson:
[03 Rotation Variations, LOD and Core-Mantle Coupling](../03-rotation-and-length-of-day.md).
All models **SYNTHETIC**; the moments of inertia, conductivity and topography are
adopted inputs.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** With $C = 8.04\times10^{37}\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{2}}$,
$\Omega = 7.292115\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$ and
$\mathrm{LOD} = 86400$ s, compute $H_3 = C\Omega$ and the angular momentum change
corresponding to a 2-ms change in the day length.

**★2.** Write the axial angular momentum budget and state, in one sentence, why
the total is conserved by any internal exchange.

**★3.** A synthetic wind field delivers $1.5\times10^{25}\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-1}}$.
Convert that to a length-of-day change in milliseconds.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Of the 2-ms change in ★1, the winds account for the amount in ★3.
Compute the residual in both milliseconds and kg m² s⁻¹, and the mean torque
needed to deliver it in 5 years.

**★★5.** Compute the electromagnetic coupling time $\tau = \mu_0\sigma dD$ for
$\sigma = 10^{3}\ \mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}$, $d = 100$ km and
$D = 2\pi R_{\text{cmb}}$ with $R_{\text{cmb}} = 3.48\times10^{6}$ m. State
whether it matches a decadal timescale, and what conductivity would.

**★★6.** Compute the topographic torque capacity
$\tau_{\max} = (\pi^{2}/2)\Delta\rho g h_0^{2}mR^{2}$ for $h_0 = 0.5$ km,
$m = 2$, $\Delta\rho = 4400\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and
$g = 10.7\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, and its ratio to the torque from ★4.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Compute the differential rotation of the inner core required to supply
the residual angular momentum of ★4, in rad s⁻¹, as a fraction of $\Omega$, and
in degrees per year. State whether that is physically plausible and what the
result implies about attributing the residual to the inner core.

**★★★8.** Design an observational test that discriminates electromagnetic from
topographic coupling as the source of a decadal LOD signal. Name the observable,
the model each mechanism requires, and the prediction that separates them.

## Notes on answers

Torque estimates must state whether they are requirements (from the budget) or
capacities (from a mechanism), and never present a capacity as an established
source.
