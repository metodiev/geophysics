# Exercises — 12-05-02 Tidal Response and the Quality Factor of the Whole Earth

Attempt before the
[solutions](./02-tidal-response-solutions.md). Lesson:
[02 Tidal Response and the Quality Factor of the Whole Earth](../02-tidal-response.md).
All models **SYNTHETIC**; the Love numbers and quality factors are adopted inputs.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the phase lag $\delta$ in degrees for $Q = 250$, using both the
exact arctangent and the small-angle replacement, and report the difference.

**★2.** A synthetic tide has stored energy $E = 8.0\times10^{16}$ J and angular
frequency $\omega = 1.4\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$. Compute the
dissipation rate in terawatts for $Q = 80$ and for $Q = 160$.

**★3.** State in one sentence why a *real* Love number cannot produce a secular
tidal recession of the Moon.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Starting from $F = k(x - (\tan\delta/\omega)\dot{x})$, derive the
identity $P = \omega E\tan\delta$ and evaluate it for $k = 4\times10^{20}$ N/m,
$x_0 = 0.2$ m, $\omega = 1.4\times10^{-4}$ rad/s and $Q = 100$.

**★★5.** Using the demonstration's single-relaxation table, compute the quality
factor the same relaxation predicts at 0.1 Hz, and state the discrepancy against
the adopted nominal seismic value of 400.

**★★6.** Compute the absorption-band exponent $\alpha$ in $Q \propto
\omega^{\alpha}$ needed to go from $Q = 200$ at the M2 frequency to $Q = 400$ at
0.1 Hz.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design an experiment that separates the ocean tide's dissipation from
the solid Earth's. Name the observables, the models they require, and the one
systematic error that dominates each.

**★★★8.** A tide-derived $Q$ is quoted as $Q = 100 \pm 30$ from a phase lag
measured to 5% on 30 cycles. Write a two-paragraph referee report on what the
uncertainty should contain, and identify which of the contributions is not
reducible by more data.

## Notes on answers

A quality factor must always be quoted with the frequency at which it was
measured, and an energy estimate with the Love number it assumed.
