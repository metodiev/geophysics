# Exercises — 12-03-03 Thermal Evolution of the Earth and Secular Cooling

Attempt before the [solutions](./03-thermal-evolution-solutions.md). Lesson:
[03 Thermal Evolution and Secular Cooling](../03-thermal-evolution.md). All
models **SYNTHETIC**; every parameter below is an adopted input.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Write the one-box mantle energy balance
$\rho c_p d\,\mathrm{d}T/\mathrm{d}t = H + q_{\text{cmb}} - q_{\text{surf}}$ and
verify that every term has the units of $\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$.

**★2.** For $H = 30\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$,
$q_{\text{cmb}} = 8\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$ and
$q_{\text{surf}} = 50\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$, compute the Urey ratio and say
whether the box is cooling or heating.

**★3.** With $\rho c_p d = 1.19\times10^{13}\ \mathrm{J\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}$,
compute the instantaneous budget cooling rate for the numbers of ★2 and express
it in both $\mathrm{K\,Gyr^{-1}}$ and $\mathrm{mK\,Ma^{-1}}$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Derive the quasi-static rate
$\mathrm{d}T/\mathrm{d}t|_{\text{qs}} = -\lambda H/(\mathrm{d}q/\mathrm{d}T)$ by
requiring the box to stay on its equilibrium curve, and evaluate it for
$H = 30\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$, $T_{1/2} = 6.0\ \mathrm{Gyr}$ and
$\mathrm{d}q/\mathrm{d}T = 0.25\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}$.

**★★5.** Show that changing the effective half-life from 4.0 to 6.0 Gyr leaves
the Urey ratio unchanged but changes the quasi-static cooling rate by a definite
factor. State the factor and explain the invariance in one sentence.

**★★6.** A model is calibrated so that $q_{\text{surf}}(T) = 55\
\mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$ at $T = 1600\ \mathrm{K}$. Using the flux sensitivity
$\mathrm{d}q/\mathrm{d}T = 0.3137\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}$, compute the
temperature at which it would instead lose $45\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$, and comment
on whether that is a large temperature change.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** The demonstration assumes a constant core flux of
$10\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$. Replace it with $q_{\text{cmb}}(t)$ growing linearly
from 0 to $20\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$ over 4.5 Gyr, compute the cumulative heat
delivered per unit area, and express it as an equivalent uniform mantle
temperature change. State whether ignoring the core's growth makes the model's
cooling rate too large or too small.

**★★★8.** Propose a synthetic observable that would distinguish
$E_a = 200\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ from $E_a = 400\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$, and
specify the data and precision needed. Explain why a present-day heat-flow value
alone cannot do it.

## Notes on answers

Answers must distinguish a *budget* rate from a *quasi-static* rate from a
*measured* rate, and must state which tuning was used to fix which parameter.
