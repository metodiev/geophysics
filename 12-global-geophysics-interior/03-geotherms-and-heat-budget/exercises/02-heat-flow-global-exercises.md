# Exercises — 12-03-02 Global Heat Flow and Its Distribution

Attempt before the [solutions](./02-heat-flow-global-solutions.md). Lesson:
[02 Heat Flow and Its Distribution](../02-heat-flow-global.md). All models
**SYNTHETIC**; the conductivities, areas and age distribution are adopted inputs.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Using the half-space law in the form $q\sqrt{t} = 391.7\
\mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}\,Ma^{0.5}}$, compute $q$ at 3, 30 and 100 Ma, and state the
units of the constant.

**★2.** Compute the thermal boundary-layer thickness $2\sqrt{\kappa t}$ at 5 and
50 Ma with $\kappa = 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$, and state what physical
assumption makes the same formula describe a subducting plate.

**★3.** Show by substitution that $q(t) = k\Delta T/\sqrt{\pi\kappa t}$ satisfies
$q\sqrt{t} = \text{const}$, and evaluate the constant for $k = 3.0\
\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, $\Delta T = 1300\ \mathrm{K}$,
$\kappa = 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Differentiate the inverted age law to show $\delta t/t = 2\,\delta q/q$,
then evaluate the age error at 100 Ma for a 5% flux error. State the units of the
result.

**★★5.** The demonstration's plate-model flux at 150 Ma is
$43.6\ \mathrm{mW\,m^{-2}}$; the half-space value is $391.7/\sqrt{150}$. Compute
the ratio and explain in two sentences why the two models may not be told apart
below 20 Ma.

**★★6.** Double the area of the 0–2 Ma bin of the demonstration's age
distribution, rescale the other bins so that the total area stays
$3.6\times10^{14}\ \mathrm{m^2}$, and recompute the oceanic heat loss. Report the
change in TW and in per cent, and state what that says about the sensitivity of
the global total to the youngest bin.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** The near-ridge deficit in the demonstration uses
$r(t) = 1 - 0.8e^{-t/8\ \mathrm{Ma}}$. Replace it with
$r(t) = 1 - 0.9e^{-t/5\ \mathrm{Ma}}$, recompute the integrated deficit, and
state which choice you would defend and what observation would settle it.

**★★★8.** Design a test that distinguishes "plate model with $d = 90\
\mathrm{km}$" from "half-space model with a suppressed flux at old ages". State
the observable, the required precision, and the confound that makes the test
harder than it looks.

## Notes on answers

Heat-flow answers must state whether they are reporting a *model* flux, a
*measured* flux or a *lost* flux. An answer that silently substitutes one for
another is wrong regardless of its arithmetic.
