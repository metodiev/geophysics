# Exercises — 24-01-05 Keeping Up: Journals, Preprints, Societies, Alerts

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./05-keeping-up-with-literature-solutions.md). The lesson is
[05 Keeping Up](../05-keeping-up-with-literature.md). All rates are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Four sources deliver $2.0$, $6.0$, $15.0$ and $0.5$ items per day. Compute
the weekly volume, the volume over $46$ working weeks, and the reading hours at
$6\ \mathrm{min}$ per item.

**★2.** For the volume of ★1 with a $4\%$ relevance prior, compute the relevant
items per week and per $46$-week year.

**★3.** A filter has precision $0.40$ and recall $0.70$. For a year with $987$
relevant items out of $16\,068$, compute the relevant items kept, the total items
kept, the reading hours at $6\ \mathrm{min}$ per item, and the relevant items
missed as a percentage of the relevant set.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A stream has $V = 500$ items/week and a $5\%$ relevance prior. A filter
keeps $60$ items/week of which $24$ are relevant. With a cap of $50$ opened items
per week, compute the relevant items reached per week (a) with the filter and
(b) without it. Give the ratio.

**★★5.** Using the demonstration's filtered stream ($31.7$ items/week, precision
$0.534$, $21.0$ relevant items/week unfiltered), compute the cap $C$ on the
*unfiltered* stream that would deliver the same number of relevant items per week
as the filtered stream at full coverage.

**★★6.** Filter (a) keeps $1456$ items/year with $777$ relevant; filter (b) keeps
$700$ items/year with $620$ relevant. Compute the annual reading hours at
$6\ \mathrm{min}$ per item for each, the marginal minutes per relevant item found
for each, and the number of relevant items filter (b) forgoes relative to (a).

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design an eight-week experiment that measures the precision of your own
alert filter. State the stream, the cap, the outcome that counts an opened item as
"useful", how you obtain the labels without circularity, and the confound that the
project's own progress introduces.

**★★★8.** Specify an experiment that separates *volume* from *fatigue* in the
opened-fraction curve. State the treatment, the unit of randomisation, the sample
size implication, the outcome, and why a within-person design is needed rather
than a between-person one.
