# Exercises — 24-01-03 Critiquing Methods, Data and Inference

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-critiquing-methods-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Critiquing Methods, Data and Inference](../03-critiquing-methods.md). All
models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** The four reproducibility-critical items have reporting rates $0.61$,
$0.69$, $0.44$ and $0.71$. Compute the joint reporting rate under an independence
assumption, and compare it with the $10.7\%$ measured in the demonstration.

**★2.** Using the weights $(1,1,3,2,3,2,3,3,2,1)$ for the ten items in the order
given in the lesson, compute the weighted audit score of a paper that reports
items $1$, $2$, $4$, $6$, $8$ and $10$ and omits the rest.

**★3.** Compute the reviewer effort, in hours and in forty-hour working weeks, for
$150$ papers each reviewed by $3$ reviewers at $4\ \mathrm{h}$ per review.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** With measured rates $0.613$, $0.693$, $0.90$ (after the improvement) and
$0.707$ for the critical items, compute the independence prediction of the joint
rate. Compare with the measured $28.0\%$ and explain the difference.

**★★5.** Take a defect rate of $0.35$, a detectability of $0.50$ when the item is
reported and $0.15$ when it is not, and an item reporting rate of $0.44$. Compute
the expected number of visible and invisible defects in $150$ papers. Then explain
why your answer differs from the demonstration's $19$ visible out of $54$ defects.

**★★6.** You want to compare mean audit scores between two journals, expecting a
difference of $10$ points with a between-paper standard deviation of $16.3$ points.
Compute the papers per group needed for $80\%$ power at a two-sided $5\%$ level.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a reporting audit of $30$ papers in your own subfield, using a
checklist derived inductively from the papers themselves rather than imposed in
advance. State how you derive the items, the coding protocol, the second coder and
the agreement target, and what you would do with items that almost no paper
reports.

**★★★8.** Specify the study that would measure the *actual* defect rate rather
than a modelled one, with access to the code and data of a set of papers. State
the unit of analysis, the outcome definition, how the assessment is kept blind to
the paper's conclusions, and the sampling bias you cannot remove.
