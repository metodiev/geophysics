# Exercises — 24-01-04 Systematic Literature Search and Reference Management

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-literature-search-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 Systematic Literature Search](../04-literature-search.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A Boolean query retrieves $108$ records from a corpus of $4000$ in which
$240$ are relevant. Of the retrieved records $40$ are relevant, and $200$ relevant
records are not retrieved. Compute the precision, the recall and $F_1$.

**★2.** At $1.5\ \mathrm{min}$ per title-and-abstract screen, compute the screening
hours for (a) the $108$-record query of ★1 and (b) a union query retrieving $1543$
records.

**★3.** Compute the number needed to screen for a precision of $10.3\%$ and for a
precision of $58.5\%$. State the difference in reading terms.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A corpus has a relevant prevalence of $6\%$. A phrase query matches
$30\%$ of relevant records and $3\%$ of non-relevant records. Compute the expected
precision by Bayes' rule, and compare it with the $40.8\%$ measured in the
demonstration, giving the standard error of the measured value.

**★★5.** A duplicate pair of titles has $10$ three-word shingles each; after one
word substitution and one adjacent transposition, $3$ shingles differ. Compute the
Jaccard similarity of the shingle sets and state, using the demonstration's
threshold table, whether a $0.50$ threshold would merge them.

**★★6.** With the union query's precision of $10.3\%$ and a screening rate of
$1.5\ \mathrm{min}$ per record, compute the screening minutes per relevant paper
found. Compare it with a $45\ \mathrm{min}$ deep read of the same paper and state
the ratio.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a systematic search protocol for a review in your subfield:
state the sources and coverage dates, three query strings with their Boolean
structure, the inclusion and exclusion criteria, the screening stages with
reviewers, the pilot size for screening agreement and the agreement statistic with
its target, and the yield accounting line you would report.

**★★★8.** Specify a study that estimates the fraction of a review's included
studies that a given query could have retrieved, using an existing review as the
gold standard. State the metric, the sampling, and the bias that the choice of
gold-standard review introduces.
