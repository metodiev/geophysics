# Exercises — 24-02-05 Abstracts, Titles and the Art of the Concrete Claim

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./05-writing-abstracts-titles-solutions.md). The lesson is
[05 Abstracts, Titles and the Art of the Concrete Claim](../05-writing-abstracts-titles.md).
All rates are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Using the demonstration's title-element rates, compute the concreteness
score of a title that names the object and the method but neither the setting nor
the scope, where the score is the number of elements present divided by four.

**★2.** An abstract is $297$ words. The limit is $250$. Compute the excess and, at
the demonstration's mean sentence length of $22$ words, the number of sentences to
delete.

**★3.** Six content flags are context, gap, method, quantitative result,
uncertainty and implication. Compute the content score of an abstract that states
context, method, quantitative result and implication but neither the gap nor the
uncertainty.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** The method flag appears in $95.8\%$ of structured abstracts and $71.2\%$ of
unstructured ones. Compute the difference in percentage points and the odds ratio.

**★★5.** Uncertainty language appears in $48.1\%$ of structured abstracts and the
ratio of uncertainty language between significant and non-significant abstracts is
$1.35$. Compute the implied rate among non-significant abstracts and compare it
with the demonstration's $35.0\%$.

**★★6.** The demonstration's mean abstract is $247$ words and the mean paper is
$6577$ words. Compute the abstract's share of the paper, and then compute how many
words of the abstract a reader sees if they read only the first and last sentences
at the mean sentence length.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study testing whether a structured abstract improves a reader's
ability to state the paper's method and its primary number. State the design, the
outcome, the sample size for a $12$-point difference on a $100$-point scale with a
between-subject standard deviation of $20$, and the blinding.

**★★★8.** Specify a prospective audit of abstract-versus-Results agreement across
your own last twelve months of writing: the unit, the coding of agreement, the
agreement statistic, and what a disagreement rate of $10\%$ would imply.
