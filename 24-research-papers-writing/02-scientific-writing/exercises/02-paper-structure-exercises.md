# Exercises — 24-02-02 Paper Structure: IMRaD and Its Variants

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-paper-structure-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Paper Structure](../02-paper-structure.md). All corpus numbers are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A manuscript has an IMRaD body of $4992$ words distributed as
$900$ Introduction, $1500$ Methods, $1200$ Results and $1392$ Discussion. Compute
each section's share of the body and the specificity index
$(\text{Methods}+\text{Results})/(\text{Introduction}+\text{Discussion})$.

**★2.** Using the demonstration's rates of $1.5$, $6.0$, $14.0$ and $4.0$ numbers
per $1000$ words for Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion, compute the
number of numeric tokens in each section of the manuscript of ★1 and the total.

**★3.** A journal caps the abstract at $250$ words. The demonstration's mean
abstract is $247 \pm 43$ words and the mean paper is $6577$ words. Compute the
abstract's share of the paper and the fraction of abstracts longer than $300$ words.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A manuscript has a Discussion of $800$ words and an Introduction of
$1400$ words. Compute the ratio and the difference, and state the diagnosis using
the demonstration's finding that $65.0\%$ of papers have a longer Discussion than
Introduction.

**★★5.** A body of $6000$ words must have a specificity index of $1.4$. Compute the
narrow budget (Methods + Results) and the wide budget (Introduction + Discussion).

**★★6.** The demonstration measures $28.7\%$ of papers with a merged Results and
Discussion, $13.7\%$ with a separate Theory section and $32.7\%$ with a setting
section, and $0.3\%$ with all three. Compute the independence prediction for all
three and state whether the variants are positively or negatively associated.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a corpus study that estimates the mean Methods share in your
subfield to a half-width of $\pm 1$ percentage point at $95\%$ confidence, using
the demonstration's between-paper standard deviation of the share ($18.7\%$) as a
planning value. State the sample size, the sampling frame, and the inclusion rule
for papers without a separable Methods section.

**★★★8.** Specify a study testing whether papers with a merged Results and
Discussion are more likely to overclaim. Define the outcome, the coding unit, the
blinding, the agreement statistic, and the confound that a merged section is more
common in short papers.
