# Exercises — 24-04-02 Designing Effective Scientific Posters

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-posters-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Designing Effective Scientific Posters](../02-posters.md). All numbers are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the maximum viewing distance for body text of $14\ \mathrm{mm}$ and
for a caption of $9\ \mathrm{mm}$, using a legibility angle of $0.0085\ \mathrm{rad}$.

**★2.** Your poster will be read from $2.0\ \mathrm{m}$. Compute the minimum text
height and compare it with a $10\ \mathrm{mm}$ body size.

**★3.** An A0 poster is $1.00\ \mathrm{m^{2}}$. At $420$ words per square metre for
$10\ \mathrm{mm}$ text, compute the capacity at $10\ \mathrm{mm}$ and at $20\
\mathrm{mm}$, and state the scaling rule.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A poster session runs $120$ minutes with a mean dwell of $2.5$ minutes.
Compute the maximum engaged visitors, the realistic figure at half occupancy, and
the engaged fraction of a modelled traffic of $250$.

**★★5.** In the demonstration's reading model, the title is read by $100\%$, figures
by $66.7\%$, captions by $36.7\%$ and body text by $22.2\%$ of $90$ visitors. Compute
the expected number of visitors who read both the figures and the body text, and
state what that implies for where the primary result should appear.

**★★6.** Your poster currently holds $700$ words at $9\ \mathrm{mm}$. Compute the
text size needed to hold $150$ words at the same area density rule, and state whether
that size is legible from $2\ \mathrm{m}$.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design an observational study measuring whether a self-contained caption
increases the time visitors spend at a poster. State the unit, the outcome, the
measurement instrument, and the confound that the poster's topic introduces.

**★★★8.** Specify a study estimating the distribution of viewing distances at a
poster session. State the measurement, the sample, the statistic you would report,
and how the result would change the legibility rule you adopt.
