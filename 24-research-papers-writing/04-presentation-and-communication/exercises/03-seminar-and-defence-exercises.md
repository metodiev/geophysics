# Exercises — 24-04-03 Seminars, Qualifying Exams and Thesis Defence

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-seminar-and-defence-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Seminars, Qualifying Exams and Thesis Defence](../03-seminar-and-defence.md).
All numbers are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A defence runs $50$ minutes with a $15$-minute talk. Compute the question
time and the number of questions that fit at $3.5$ minutes per answer and at $1.5$
minutes per answer.

**★2.** Using the coverage model $1 - e^{-n/12}$, compute coverage for $12$, $24$ and
$36$ prepared answers, and the marginal gain from $24$ to $36$.

**★3.** Write the three-part answer (direct answer, reason, limit) for the question
"why did you choose that frequency band?" for a method you know.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** The demonstration's two lowest-weight areas carry $8\%$ each. Compute the
probability that at least one of them appears in a panel of $6$ questions, and in a
panel of $12$.

**★★5.** You answer at an average of $4.5$ minutes per question in a $30$-minute
question period. Compute the number of questions answered, and the number you would
answer at $2.5$ minutes. Express the difference as a fraction of a plausible panel
agenda of $10$ questions.

**★★6.** An examiner asserts a premise your work contradicts. Write the four
sentences you would use: the correction, the quantitative consequence, the question
you think they meant, and your answer to it.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study measuring whether a rehearsal with an instructed-unfriendly
audience reduces the number of questions a candidate fails to answer in a defence.
State the unit, the outcome, the design, the sample size for a difference of $1.5$
unanswered questions with a standard deviation of $1.8$ questions, and the ethical
constraint on randomising candidates.

**★★★8.** Specify a study estimating the distribution of question areas across
defences in your department. State the sampling, the coding unit, the agreement
statistic, and how you would use the result to set a candidate's preparation
priority.
