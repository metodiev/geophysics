# Exercises — 24-03-02 Hypotheses, Falsifiability and Testing in Earth Science

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-hypothesis-and-falsifiability-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Hypotheses and Falsifiability](../02-hypothesis-and-falsifiability.md). All
simulated numbers are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Write $H_0$, $H_1$ and one falsifier for the claim that the northern block
is faster than the southern block, where the measurement is a shear-wave velocity
with a stated uncertainty.

**★2.** A test at $\alpha = 0.05$ is run $4000$ times on null data and produces
$187$ significant results. Compute the observed false-positive rate and its
standard error.

**★3.** Compute the family-wise error rate for $20$ independent null tests at
$\alpha = 0.05$ exactly, and compare it with the demonstration's simulated
$64.2\%$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** At $n = 32$ per group the demonstration reports power of $13.2\%$ at
$d = 0.20$ and $88.8\%$ at $d = 0.80$. Using the analytic power formula with the
normal approximation, compute the power at both effect sizes and compare.

**★★5.** Compute the number of defensible choices $k$ at which the flexibility
error rate first exceeds $90\%$ under the null, and state the practical implication
for a study with eight processing decisions.

**★★6.** A study runs one primary test and $12$ secondary tests at
$\alpha = 0.05$. Compute the family-wise error rate for the secondary family, the
Bonferroni threshold, and the power loss at $n = 32$ for $d = 0.5$ compared with
the uncorrected threshold (use the normal approximation).

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a pre-registration for a field experiment that fixes the six
points of the lesson's §6. State the primary outcome, the sample size with its
justification, the decision rules, and how you would handle a mid-campaign
instrument failure so that it does not become a degree of freedom.

**★★★8.** Specify a study measuring whether pre-registration reduces the
flexibility error rate in a subfield. State the unit, the outcome, how you recover
the number of analyses actually run, and the two biases you cannot remove.
