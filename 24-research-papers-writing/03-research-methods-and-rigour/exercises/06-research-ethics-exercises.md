# Exercises — 24-03-06 Research Ethics, Plagiarism, Authorship and Integrity

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./06-research-ethics-solutions.md). The lesson is
[06 Research Ethics](../06-research-ethics.md). All similarity values and
contribution matrices are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** State the difference between plagiarism and redundant publication, with an
example of each that could occur in an honest project.

**★2.** Two texts have a $5$-gram shingle Jaccard of $0.62$ and a bag-of-words
cosine of $0.94$. State which measure indicates sequence reuse and which indicates
vocabulary reuse, and what each number licenses you to do.

**★3.** Explain why a similarity score is a screen rather than a finding, in two
sentences.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Increasing substitution from $10\%$ to $30\%$ drops the shingle Jaccard
from $0.385$ to $0.059$ while the cosine falls only from $0.965$ to $0.908$.
Compute the ratio of the two drops and explain the difference mechanistically.

**★★5.** A contribution matrix gives contributors with $4$, $3$, $3$, $2$, $2$ and
$1$ criteria met. Compute the author list under the rules $\geq 1$, $\geq 2$,
$\geq 3$ and "all $4$" criteria, and compute the mean criteria per author in each
case.

**★★6.** A team of three authors adds a fourth as a courtesy. Compute the change in
mean criteria per author if the three had $4$, $3$ and $3$ criteria and the fourth
has $0$, and state what information the author list loses.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study that estimates the rate of undisclosed text reuse in a
subfield using similarity screening plus manual reading. State the corpus, the
screening threshold with both error rates, the reading protocol, the coding of
outcomes, and the reason the estimate is a lower bound.

**★★★8.** Specify a study testing whether a written authorship criterion agreed
before a project changes the composition of author lists. State the unit, the
outcome, the comparison, and the confound that projects with written agreements are
also better managed in general.
