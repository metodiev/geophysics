# Exercises — 24-03-03 Designing Observational, Experimental and Modelling Studies

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-study-design-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Designing Studies](../03-study-design.md). All numbers are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the sample size per group for $80\%$ power at $\alpha = 0.05$ with
a standardised effect of $d = 0.40$. Then compute it for $d = 0.20$ and state the
ratio.

**★2.** For $n = 64$ per group and $d = 0.50$, compute the standard error of the
standardised difference and the power using the normal approximation.

**★3.** A measurement has reliability $\lambda = 0.75$ and a true slope of $1.60$.
Compute the observed slope and the bias as a percentage of the truth.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** The demonstration's power curve reaches $81.0\%$ at $n = 64$ and $97.6\%$
at $n = 128$. Compute the marginal power bought per additional station in each
interval and state which is the better investment.

**★★5.** A confounder loads on both the predictor and the outcome with loading
$0.40$. Compute the expected unadjusted regression slope when the true direct
effect is zero, and state whether the association would be detected at $n = 200$
with a standard error of $0.07$.

**★★6.** An observed slope is $0.42$ with a measured reliability of $0.60$.
Compute the corrected slope estimate and state what additional assumption the
correction requires.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study to detect a $5\%$ change in shear-wave velocity between two
arrays, given a pilot per-station standard deviation of $6\%$ and $20$ stations per
array. State whether the design is adequate, what would fix it, and the assumptions
in the calculation.

**★★★8.** Specify a design that separates a confounded association in an
observational geophysical study. State the confounder list, how each is measured,
the identification assumption, and the sensitivity analysis if one confounder is
unmeasurable.
