# Exercises — 24-05-01 Writing a Compelling Research Proposal

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-research-proposal-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Writing a Compelling Research Proposal](../01-research-proposal.md). All numbers
are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A scheme weights novelty $0.25$, feasibility $0.25$, methods $0.20$, impact
$0.20$ and team $0.10$. A proposal scores $4$, $5$, $3$, $4$ and $3$ on those
criteria out of $5$. Compute its weighted score.

**★2.** Compute the marginal score gain of one extra point on each of the five
criteria of ★1, and identify the criteria where a point is worth most.

**★3.** The score at the funding boundary is $3.8088$ and the next proposal scores
$3.7941$. Compute the margin in points and as a percentage of a $5$-point scale.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Two schemes differ and produce a typical score shift of $0.08$ points.
Using the margin of ★3, compute the ratio of shift to margin and state what it implies
about the decision.

**★★5.** A proposal scores $3.60$ under the science-led weights of the lesson and its
author can improve two criteria by one point each: novelty or impact. Compute the new
score for each choice under both the science-led and the impact-led weightings, and
state which improvement is better under each.

**★★6.** The funding line in the lesson is at the top $25\%$ of $40$ proposals.
Compute the number funded, and the number of proposals that must be funded for the
margin at the line to be $0.05$ points if consecutive scores differ by $0.015$ on
average.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study that measures whether proposals aligned to a published
scoring rubric score higher than equally strong proposals that are not aligned. State
the unit, the design, the outcome, the sample size for a $0.25$-point difference with
a standard deviation of $0.5$ points, and the confound that alignment correlates with
experience.

**★★★8.** Specify a study estimating the effect of panel weighting on funding
outcomes using historical data. State the data you would need, the identification
problem, and why randomisation of proposals to panels is not available.
