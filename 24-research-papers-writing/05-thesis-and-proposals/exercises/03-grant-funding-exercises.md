# Exercises — 24-05-03 Understanding Grant Funding and Review Criteria

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./03-grant-funding-solutions.md). The lesson is
[03 Understanding Grant Funding and Review Criteria](../03-grant-funding.md). All
numbers are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A scheme funds at $18\%$ with $250$ applications. Compute the number funded
and the expected number of applications per award.

**★2.** Per-reviewer noise is $0.60$ points and each application receives $9$ reviews.
Compute the standard error of the panel mean and the $90\%$ band around the boundary.

**★3.** The band of ★2 contains a set of applications. Compute how many reviewers are
needed to halve the band, and the resulting standard error.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** With $400$ applications and $80$ funded, the boundary is at rank $80$. If
$21.8\%$ of applications lie inside a $\pm 0.26$-point band, compute the number inside
and the number of those that are funded.

**★★5.** Success probabilities are $p = 0.10$ for a first submission and $p = 0.25$
for a revised resubmission. Compute the expected number of submissions under each
strategy and the reduction achieved by making revision the default.

**★★6.** Effort is $25$ days per application, $300$ applications are submitted, and
$60$ are funded. Compute the total effort, the effort per funded project, and the
fraction of effort spent on unsuccessful applications.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study measuring whether structured panel discussion narrows the
band of uncertain decisions. State the unit, the outcome, the design, and the ethical
constraint on manipulating a real funding panel.

**★★★8.** Specify an analysis of the relationship between proposal revision and
subsequent success using scheme records. State the data, the identification problem
(revised proposals differ from unrevised ones), and the design that would isolate the
effect of revision.
