# Exercises — 24-05-02 Thesis Structure, Chapters and the Contribution Statement

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-thesis-structure-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Thesis Structure](../02-thesis-structure.md). All numbers are **SYNTHETIC**
teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A thesis has $48\,000$ words and pages hold $400$ words. Compute the page
count and the words to cut against a limit of $110$ pages.

**★2.** Chapter 3 has $12\,000$ words and chapter 4 has $10\,000$ words with a $30\%$
overlap of the latter. Compute the repeated word count and the fraction of the
thesis's $69\,000$ words it represents.

**★3.** A contribution is supported by chapters with $12\,000$ and $10\,000$ words. At
a support threshold of $15\,000$ words, state whether it is supported and by how much
it clears the threshold.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Five claimed contributions are supported by word counts of $31\,000$,
$21\,000$, $23\,000$, $9000$ and $4000$. Compute the mean and median support, and the
fraction of contributions supported at a $15\,000$-word threshold.

**★★5.** A thesis has $69\,000$ words with $14.6\%$ redundancy and a $5000$-word cut
is required. Compute the words remaining if the redundancy is removed by reference,
and state whether the target is met.

**★★6.** A chapter of $11\,000$ words is drafted at $450$ words per day and then
revised in three passes cutting $12\%$, $8\%$ and $5\%$. Compute the drafting days,
the final word count and the total reduction in words.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study measuring whether a pre-registered contribution statement
(reference at the start of the thesis) improves examiner agreement on what the thesis
contributes. State the unit, the outcome, the agreement statistic, and the ethical
constraint on randomising candidates.

**★★★8.** Specify an analysis of redundancy in a corpus of theses: the sampling, the
measurement of overlap, the agreement statistic, and the two reasons a high overlap
may be legitimate.
