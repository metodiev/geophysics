# Exercises — 06-04-07 Focal Mechanisms from First Motions and Waveform Inversion

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./07-focal-mechanisms-solutions.md). The lesson is
[07 Focal Mechanisms](../07-focal-mechanisms.md). All models are **SYNTHETIC**
teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** For the synthetic double couple with $M_0 = 3.5\times10^{17}\ \mathrm{N\,m}$
the tensor has $M_{NN} = +1.75\times10^{17}$, $M_{EE} = +1.75\times10^{17}$,
$M_{UU} = -3.5\times10^{17}$ and $M_{NE} = -1.75\times10^{17}\ \mathrm{N\,m}$.
Compute $\mathbf{n}^{T}\mathbf{M}\mathbf{n}$ for the station direction
$\mathbf{n} = (0.058, -0.280, 0.958)$ and state its polarity.

**★2.** Show from those components that the trace vanishes.

**★3.** State the physical meaning of the three eigenvalues of a double-couple
moment tensor.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Show that $(225^\circ, 45^\circ, -90^\circ)$ and
$(45^\circ, 135^\circ, 90^\circ)$ describe the same mechanism, and explain what
that implies for comparing two published mechanisms.

**★★5.** The grid contains 72 strikes × 17 dips × 71 rakes = 86 904 mechanisms and
20 of them reproduce all 24 polarities exactly. Express the fraction as a
percentage, and explain why a *lower* percentage would not necessarily mean a
better determined mechanism.

**★★6.** A mixed tensor has eigenvalues $(0.5252, 0.2816, -0.3568)$ in units of
its largest component, so its isotropic part is $0.15$ and its deviatoric
eigenvalues are $(0.3752, 0.1316, -0.5068)$. Compute $a = d_1 + 2d_2$ and
$b = -d_2$, and verify that $a(1,0,-1) + b(2,-1,-1)$ reproduces
$(d_1, d_2, d_3)$.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** A polarity inversion and a waveform inversion are applied to the same
event and give mechanisms differing by 15° in strike. Design the test that
decides whether the difference is significant, and state what data it needs.

**★★★8.** Design an experiment that measures how much the *station distribution*
inflates the admissible set of mechanisms, and predict the direction of the
change relative to the uniform-coverage case used in the lesson.
