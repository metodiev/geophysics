# Exercises — 06-04-06 Energy Release, Moment Calculations and Scaling

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./06-energy-moment-calculations-solutions.md). The lesson is
[06 Energy Release, Moment Calculations and Scaling](../06-energy-moment-calculations.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** With the demonstration's path factor
$4\pi\rho\beta^{3}R = 1.4547\times10^{20}$ and $a = 0.6325$, compute the spectral
plateau for $M_0 = 10^{17}\ \mathrm{N\,m}$ and the corresponding $M_w$.

**★2.** Show that $\int_{0}^{\infty}(1-s)^{2}e^{-2s}\,\mathrm{d}s = \tfrac14$.

**★3.** State the units of $M_0$, $E_R$, $\tau_a$ and $\Omega_0$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** For $M_0 = 10^{16}\ \mathrm{N\,m}$, $r = 4.68\ \mathrm{km}$ and
$E_R = 3.0066\times10^{9}\ \mathrm{J}$, compute $\tau_a = \mu E_R/M_0$ with
$\mu = 3.308\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{Pa}$, compute
$\Delta\sigma = \tfrac{7}{16}M_0/r^{3}$, and give the ratio.

**★★5.** Using
$\text{fraction} = \tfrac{2}{\pi}[\arctan X - X/(1+X^{2})]$ with $X = f_{\text{lp}}/f_c$,
compute the fraction of the pulse energy kept by a 2 Hz low-pass for
$f_c = 0.2\ \mathrm{Hz}$.

**★★6.** Derive the band-fraction formula from the velocity spectrum
$|V(f)| = 2\pi f A/(1 + (2\pi f\tau)^{2})$ and the total
$\int v^{2}\mathrm{d}t = A^{2}/(4\tau^{3})$.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study that measures
$\mathrm{d}\log_{10}E_R/\mathrm{d}M_w$ from real data. State the band correction
and the attenuation correction it needs, and describe the two ways in which the
measured slope can come out wrong.

**★★★8.** The energy integral diverges for an $f^{-2}$ displacement spectrum
without a high-frequency rolloff. Propose an operational definition of $E_R$ that
is finite and reproducible, and state what must be reported with it.
