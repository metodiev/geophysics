# Exercises — 06-08-04 Anisotropy from Receiver Functions and Shear-Wave Splitting

Attempt before the [solutions](./04-anisotropy-receiver-functions-solutions.md).
Lesson: [04 Anisotropy](../04-anisotropy-receiver-functions.md). SYNTHETIC values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** For $v_s = 4.20\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, $\gamma = 3\%$,
$L = 80\ \mathrm{km}$, compute $v_{\text{fast}}$, $v_{\text{slow}}$ and the
splitting delay $\delta t$.

**★2.** State the nodes and maxima of $A(\varphi) = \sin 2(\varphi-30^\circ)$
over $0^\circ\le\varphi<180^\circ$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** Derive $\delta t = 2\gamma L/(v_s(1-\gamma^{2}))$ from the two shear
speeds, and give the small-$\gamma$ limit. State the neglected term.

**★★4.** A splitting delay of $1.20\ \mathrm{s}$ is measured with
$v_s = 4.40\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. Compute $\gamma L$ and give three
$(L,\gamma)$ pairs that fit.

**★★5.** The demonstration's condition number is $319$ for clustered azimuths and
$1.0$ for full coverage. Explain what the condition number measures and why a
value near 1 means the two model types are separable.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** Using the demonstration's Monte Carlo ($0.839\pm0.671$ clustered,
$0.004\pm0.002$ full), write the two-sentence conclusion a careful author would
publish, and the sentence an over-claiming author might write. Identify the
difference.

**★★★7.** Design a station-network argument: given that the ambiguity is an
azimuthal-coverage problem, specify how many stations and what azimuthal spread
would be needed to separate a $360^\circ$ dip term from a $180^\circ$ anisotropy
term at the $2\sigma$ level. State the assumptions your answer relies on.
