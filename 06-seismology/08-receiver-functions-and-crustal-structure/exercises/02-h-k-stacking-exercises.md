# Exercises — 06-08-02 H-K Stacking and Crustal Thickness Estimation

Attempt before the [solutions](./02-h-k-stacking-solutions.md). Lesson:
[02 H-K Stacking](../02-h-k-stacking.md). All models **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute $t_{Ps}$, $t_{PpPs}$ and $t_{PpSs}$ for $H = 35\ \mathrm{km}$,
$v_p = 6.20\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, $k = 1.70$, $p = 0.060\ \mathrm{s\,km^{-1}}$.

**★2.** Using the leg-counting table, explain in one sentence why
$t_{PpPs} = t_{Ps} + 2Hq_p$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★3.** Derive $\partial t_{PpPs}/\partial p^{2}$ and evaluate it for ★1. State
its sign and why it differs from the primary's.

**★★4.** The demonstration's 95% set is $H\in[29.25,30.75]\ \mathrm{km}$ and
$k\in[1.700,1.750]$ on a $45\times27$ grid. A referee asks for $H$ to
$\pm0.1\ \mathrm{km}$. Write a two-sentence reply.

**★★5.** Two models score $9.708$ and $8.445$. Express the difference as a
percentage and argue whether it justifies preferring the first.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★6.** The demonstration uses weights $(0.7,0.2,0.1)$ and subtracts the third
term. Design a test that decides whether the recovered $(H,k)$ is sensitive to
the weights, and state exactly what you would report.

**★★★7.** With noise at 16% of the field rms, 60 realisations give
$H = 30.02 \pm 0.14\ \mathrm{km}$, while the 95% set spans $1.50\ \mathrm{km}$.
Explain what limits the answer at this noise level, and compute the noise level at
which the two limits would become comparable.
