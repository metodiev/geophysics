# Exercises — 06-09-09 Structural and Stratigraphic Interpretation

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./09-interpretation-reflection-solutions.md). The lesson is
[09 Interpretation](../09-interpretation-reflection.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A fault throws a marker by $15\ \mathrm{ms}$ in two-way time. Convert
that to a throw in metres for $v = 3200\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and compare it with
the vertical resolution scale $\lambda/4$ for $f_0 = 30\ \mathrm{Hz}$.

**★2.** The demonstration places a marker at $500\ \mathrm{m}$ depth with an
assumed constant $v = 3000\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Verify the printed two-way time
of $333.3\ \mathrm{ms}$.

**★3.** The demonstration's coherency attribute uses a 5-trace window with trace
spacing $dx = 25\ \mathrm{m}$, and it reports the minimum-coherency trace exactly
at the fault. Compute the lateral uncertainty in the fault position implied by
the window, and compare it with $\lambda/4 = 25\ \mathrm{m}$.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Explain why a coherence/similarity attribute with a window of $N$
traces can localise a lateral discontinuity only to about half the window width,
and state how you would reduce the uncertainty without changing the trace
spacing.

**★★5.** A reservoir top is mapped at $t_0 = 2.4\ \mathrm{s}$ and converted with
$v = 3000\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Compute the depth. Then compute the depth
uncertainty arising from (i) a $4\%$ velocity uncertainty and (ii) a
$\pm2\ \mathrm{ms}$ time-picking uncertainty, and state which dominates.

**★★6.** Write the full uncertainty statement for the reservoir depth of ★5,
including the quantisation of the time grid if the seismic sample interval is
$4\ \mathrm{ms}$ (two-way time), and state what additional information would
reduce the dominant term.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a QC procedure for a structural interpretation that would catch
a **phase error** (a wavelet polarity or rotation error) before it propagates
into a fault network. State the observable, the diagnostic, the threshold, and
the evidence that distinguishes a phase error from a genuine structural
complexity.

**★★★8.** The demonstration shows that even with no noise the picked throw is
quantised by the depth sample ($40.0\ \mathrm{m}$), and that with a noise rms of
$0.10$ the picked throw is $55\ \mathrm{m}$ against a true $40\ \mathrm{m}$.
Specify the picking and reporting protocol that would make this uncertainty
visible in a final map, and state the threshold above which you would refuse to
publish a throw value.
