# Exercises — 07-06-02 Tidal Corrections in Precise Measurements

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-tidal-corrections-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Tidal Corrections](../02-tidal-corrections.md). **All data in this module are
SYNTHETIC.**

Constants: the simple-correction amplitude constant is
$95.11\ \mathrm{\mu Gal}$ (at the equator, body in the equatorial plane, for the
stated standard constants); $\delta = 1.1530$; the rounded conventional
barometric factor is $-0.30\ \mathrm{\mu Gal\,hPa^{-1}}$;
$1\ \mathrm{\mu Gal} = 10^{-8}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Compute the simple correction's amplitude at $\varphi = 60^\circ$ and
state the ratio of the tide to a $20\ \mathrm{\mu Gal}$ target. Then state the
relative accuracy the correction would need to keep its residual below
$1\ \mathrm{\mu Gal}$.

**★2.** For $h_2 = 0.6140$ and $k_2 = 0.3020$, compute $\delta$ and the
percentage change in a fitted M2 amplitude of $42.0\ \mathrm{\mu Gal}$ relative to
$\delta = 1.1530$.

**★3.** A station is occupied for 4 hours and the pressure falls by
$8\ \mathrm{hPa}$. Compute the gravity change caused by the pressure alone and
compare it with the M2 amplitude at $45^\circ$N ($42.4\ \mathrm{\mu Gal}$).

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** Using the demonstration's latitude table
($0^\circ$: M2-only rms error $54.940$, four-constituent $8.208$;
$30^\circ$: $48.418$ / $4.398$; $45^\circ$: $49.890$ / $5.590$;
$60^\circ$: $58.888$ / $10.145$), decide which latitudes a *four-constituent*
model serves at the $1\ \mathrm{\mu Gal}$ level and state what that implies for a
global microgravity survey.

**★★5.** A load of $20\%$ of the body M2 amplitude lags the body tide by
$60^\circ$. Compute the fitted M2 amplitude change as a fraction, and the phase
of the resulting constituent relative to the body tide.

**★★6.** A 6-hour loop is corrected with a tide model, and the fitted drift is
$5.5\ \mathrm{\mu Gal\,h^{-1}}$. The demonstration's table shows that a $10\%$
tide-model error moves the fitted drift by about $\pm2.6\
\mathrm{\mu Gal\,h^{-1}}$ (sign following the sign of the error). State the
possible range of the true drift and what measurement would resolve it.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study that would separate the body tide, the ocean load and a
local lateral-structure anomaly using a network of gravimeters. Specify the
network geometry, the load model, the synthetic truth, the forward operator, the
diagnostic, and the degeneracy you would still have.

**★★★8.** The demonstration finds the simple model's error largest at the
equator. Construct the argument that predicts *which* omission dominates at each
latitude, and state what observation would falsify your argument. A prediction
without a falsification statement is incomplete.
