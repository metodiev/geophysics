# Exercises — 07-03-01 Interpreting Anomalies: Geometry, Depth and Amplitude Rules

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./01-anomaly-interpretation-solutions.md). The lesson is
[01 Interpreting Anomalies](../01-anomaly-interpretation.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values; use
$G = 6.67430\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m^{3}\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ and
$1\ \mathrm{mGal} = 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Derive $x_{1/2} = z\sqrt{2^{2/3}-1}$ for the sphere and evaluate the
coefficient to six decimal places. State what the half-width of a horizontal
cylinder is, and why no factor appears.

**★2.** A cylinder anomaly has a half-width of $300\ \mathrm{m}$. Give the depth
under the cylinder rule and under the sphere rule, and the percentage difference.

**★3.** State the amplitude-versus-depth exponent for a sphere, a horizontal
cylinder and an infinite sheet, and explain in one sentence why the sheet has
none.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A sphere of radius $400\ \mathrm{m}$ and
$\Delta\rho = 250\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ lies at $900\ \mathrm{m}$ depth. Compute
the peak anomaly in mGal, the half-width, and the maximum value of
$\left|\mathrm{d}g/\mathrm{d}x\right|$ and the offset at which it occurs.

**★★5.** Using the family table in §3.5 of the lesson, find the mass required to
produce $0.30\ \mathrm{mGal}$ from a body centred at $8\ \mathrm{km}$, and the
radius at $\Delta\rho = 300\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Comment on whether the
point-mass approximation is self-consistent.

**★★6.** A colleague reports a depth of $800\ \mathrm{m}$ from a half-width of
$613\ \mathrm{m}$ using the sphere rule. Show the arithmetic, and give the depth
the cylinder rule would imply for the same profile.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Two bodies give the same peak amplitude: a cylinder of radius
$200\ \mathrm{m}$ at $500\ \mathrm{m}$ and a sphere at an unknown depth with the
same density contrast. Compute the sphere's depth, and then specify a field
measurement — station spacing, profile length and the quantity to be fitted —
that would decide which body is present. State the discriminating feature and the
noise level at which it fails.

**★★★8.** A profile is sampled at $100\ \mathrm{m}$ spacing over a $3\ \mathrm{km}$
line, with $0.03\ \mathrm{mGal}$ rms noise. Estimate the uncertainty of the
half-width, propagate it to a depth uncertainty under both rules, and state the
assumption about the regional field that your answer requires.
