# Exercises — 07-04-04 GNSS for Tectonics, Subsidence and Sea Level

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-gnss-geodesy-applications-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 GNSS Geodesy Applications](../04-gnss-geodesy-applications.md). All models and
data are **SYNTHETIC**.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Write the design matrix for a constant plus linear trend plus annual sine
and cosine, and state the unit of the velocity coefficient when $t$ is in years.

**★2.** Explain in two sentences why a formal velocity error computed under a
white-noise assumption is not an uncertainty.

**★3.** State the sign convention relating a tide-gauge trend, vertical land
motion and absolute sea-level rate.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A $3$-year daily record has a fitted velocity of $-2.5\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$
with a white-noise formal error of $0.03\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$. Using the
lesson's inflation factors, give a realistic uncertainty, and state what would
change your answer.

**★★5.** Using the record-length table, estimate the record length needed to reach
$0.1\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ under flicker noise, and state the scaling exponent you
used.

**★★6.** A $20\ \mathrm{km}$ baseline has $0.4\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ velocity
uncertainty on each station. Compute the strain-rate uncertainty from a single
difference, and state whether a $10\ \mathrm{nstrain\,yr^{-1}}$ signal is
resolvable.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a four-station array that separates strain from rotation, state
the geometric condition that makes the separation possible, and compute the
resolution for $0.5\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ station velocities over $50\ \mathrm{km}$.

**★★★8.** A five-year vertical series gives $-1.2\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$ with a
white-noise formal error of $0.08\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$. Combine it with a
tide-gauge trend of $+2.0\ \mathrm{mm\,yr^{-1}}$, give the absolute sea-level rate
with a defensible uncertainty, and state whether the sign is resolved.
