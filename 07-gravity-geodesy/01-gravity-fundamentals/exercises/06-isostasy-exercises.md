# Exercises — 07-01-06 Isostasy: Pratt, Airy and Flexural Models

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./06-isostasy-solutions.md). The lesson is
[06 Isostasy: Pratt, Airy and Flexural Models](../06-isostasy.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC**. Use $G = 6.67430\times10^{-11}\
\mathrm{m^{3}\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$, $1\ \mathrm{mGal} = 10^{-5}\
\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $g = 9.81\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ where needed.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** A SYNTHETIC plateau is $h = 1200\ \mathrm{m}$ high with
$\rho_{c} = 2650$ and $\rho_{m} = 3250\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Compute the Airy root
and the mass deficit per unit area.

**★2.** For the plateau of ★1, compute the predicted Bouguer anomaly of a perfectly
compensated plateau in two ways (from the slab term and from the root deficit) and
show they agree.

**★3.** For Pratt compensation with $\rho_{0} = 2700\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and
$D = 35\ \mathrm{km}$, compute $\rho(h)$ for $h = 2000\ \mathrm{m}$ and the
resulting deficit per unit area. Compare with the Airy deficit at the same
topography and state the percentage difference.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** The root of ★1 is a cylinder of radius $R = 80\ \mathrm{km}$. Compute the
exact attraction of the cylinder, the slab value, and the unexplained residual in
mGal and as a percentage of the compensation.

**★★5.** A SYNTHETIC elastic plate has $E = 7.0\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{Pa}$,
$\nu = 0.25$, $T_{e} = 15\ \mathrm{km}$ and $\Delta\rho = 400\
\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Compute $D$, the flexural parameter $\alpha$, the transition
wavelength $2\pi\alpha$, and the compensation fraction for loads of $\lambda = 100$,
$200$ and $400\ \mathrm{km}$.

**★★6.** A survey measures a Bouguer anomaly of $-200\ \mathrm{mGal}$ over a
plateau $2\ \mathrm{km}$ high. Assuming perfect local compensation (thin slab),
compute the required $\Delta\rho\,t$ product and the crustal thickness for
$\Delta\rho = 400$, $600$ and $800\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. State how much the answer
changes across that range.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a study that would distinguish local Airy compensation from
flexural support under a SYNTHETIC $4\ \mathrm{km}$ plateau. Specify the data, the
predicted *difference* between the models, the parameter you would estimate, and
the observation that would falsify each model.

**★★★8.** Gravity alone cannot separate $\Delta\rho$ from $t$ (question ★★6).
Specify how you would combine the gravity data with an independent seismic
constraint, state the quantity each data set constrains, and give the arithmetic
that shows how the joint answer is better than either alone.

**★★★9.** Design a test of whether a SYNTHETIC region is in isostatic equilibrium,
including a control that would demonstrate your method can detect equilibrium when
it exists. State which error source would most easily masquerade as disequilibrium,
and how you would rule it out.
