# Exercises — 18-08-04 Reporting Inversion Results Responsibly

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./04-reporting-inversion-solutions.md). The lesson is
[04 Reporting Inversion Results Responsibly](../04-reporting-inversion.md). All
models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** From the weight sweep, state the model error at the smallest and largest
$\lambda$ tested, and at the best $\lambda$. Draw the one-line conclusion.

**★2.** Write the eight lines of the report template and, for each, say whether
the number is measured, modelled or chosen.

**★3.** State the discrepancy principle in one sentence and the quantity it needs
that the L-curve does not.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A report states "the inversion found a body at 12.25 km with amplitude
0.22". Using the admitted-range table, rewrite the statement so that it is
supported by the numbers, and state what has been added.

**★★5.** The discrepancy choice ($\lambda=30$) coincides on this grid with the
best-error choice. Explain why this agreement is not a proof that the
discrepancy principle finds the best model, and design a test that would show it
failing.

**★★6.** You are asked to review a paper whose inversion section contains one
figure, one weight ("$\lambda$ chosen by trial and error") and no misfit. List
the four questions you would ask, and the answer you would need for each.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** Design a reporting standard for a trans-dimensional (reversible-jump)
inversion, in which the number of parameters is itself unknown. Specify what
replaces each line of the eight-line template and why the replacements cannot be
naive translations. A specification that could not be executed is a failed
answer.

**★★★8.** Construct a report that satisfies every line of the template and is
still misleading, and state the additional test a careful reader should demand.
