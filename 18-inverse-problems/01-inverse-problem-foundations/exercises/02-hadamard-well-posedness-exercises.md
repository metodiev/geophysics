# Exercises — 18-01-02 Hadamard's Conditions: Existence, Uniqueness, Stability

Attempt these before reading the
[solutions](./02-hadamard-well-posedness-solutions.md). The lesson is
[02 Hadamard's Conditions](../02-hadamard-well-posedness.md). All models are
**SYNTHETIC** teaching values.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** State Hadamard's three conditions. For each, name the kind of geophysical
situation in which it typically fails.

**★2.** A layered model has $M = 12$ layers of $1\ \mathrm{km}$ and only a surface
gravity reading. Show that $G$ has rank $1$ and state the dimension of the null
space. Repeat for $M = 12$ with $5$ independent borehole levels added.

**★3.** Using $c = 2\pi G_N h = 0.04193586\ \mathrm{mGal}$ per
$(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}})$ per layer, compute the surface gravity of a stack of
$12$ layers each with $\rho = 100\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Then compute it for
$\rho_k = 100 + 50(-1)^{k}$ and comment.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** A least-squares fit of a 3-parameter shallow model to data generated by
two masses at $8$ and $12\ \mathrm{km}$ leaves a misfit of
$1.537876\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{mGal}$ while the noise norm is
$8.4\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{mGal}$. (a) Compute the ratio. (b) State whether the
inverse problem has a solution and why. (c) Give the two changes to the model
space that would be needed.

**★★5.** The measured differentiation error of $f(t)=\sin t$ at $t=1$ with sample
error $10^{-6}$ is $8.994\times10^{-4}$ at $h = 10^{-1}$ and
$1.425\times10^{-5}$ at $h = 10^{-2}$, then $2.945\times10^{-4}$ at $h = 10^{-3}$.
(a) Identify which term dominates in each case. (b) Derive the optimal $h$ from
$\cos(1)h^{2}/6 = \varepsilon/h$ and compare with the observed minimum.

**★★6.** The refinement table gives $\kappa = 1.75\times10^{3}$ at $M=4$ and
$3.92\times10^{22}$ at $M = 64$, having peaked at $8.34\times10^{25}$ at $M=32$.
Explain the non-monotonicity at $M = 64$ with the help of
$\varepsilon\sigma_{\max} = 1.4\times10^{-27}$.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★7.** You are asked for the "null space" of a compact-operator
discretisation whose relative singular values are
$1,\ 10^{-1},\ 10^{-2.5},\ 10^{-4},\ 10^{-6},\ 10^{-9},\ 10^{-13},\ 10^{-17}$.
Write a short specification for the answer you will give: what you will report,
how you will choose the tolerance from a stated data precision, and how you will
express the sensitivity of your conclusion to that choice.

**★★★8.** Design a measurement plan for a 12-layer density profile that is
guaranteed to have a null space of dimension at most 2, using only measurements
from the list: surface gravity, borehole gravity at a chosen depth, and a
vertical gravity gradient. State the rows of $G$ you would use, count the rank,
and state the one assumption that would invalidate the count.
