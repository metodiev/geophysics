# Exercises — 18-06-06 Decision-Making Under Uncertainty and Value of Information

Lesson: [06 Decision-Making Under Uncertainty and Value of Information](../06-decision-under-uncertainty.md).
Attempt these before reading the [solutions](./06-decision-under-uncertainty-solutions.md).
All models are **SYNTHETIC** teaching values in dimensionless currency units.

## ★ Foundational

**★1.** Derive the break-even volume $V^*$ for a binary decision with value
$P\,V - C$ for acting and $0$ for not acting, and state the decision rule in terms of
$\mathbb{E}[V]$.

**★2.** Explain in two sentences why the expected value of perfect information cannot
be negative, using $\mathbb{E}[\max_a \cdot] \ge \max_a \mathbb{E}[\cdot]$.

**★3.** For a protection decision costing $C = 5$ with loss $L = 50$, compute the
cost-loss ratio and state the decision rule.

## ★★ Applied

**★★4.** The SYNTHETIC posterior on the volume is lognormal with median $8$ and
log-standard-deviation $0.6$. Compute $\mathbb{E}[V]$, the value of drilling at a
cost of $20$ with a value of $5$ per unit, and the break-even volume. State whether
the action is taken.

**★★5.** Under exponential utility with $A = 0.1$ per currency unit, the
demonstration reports a certainty equivalent of $9.3700$ for drilling, against an
expected value of $27.8887$. Interpret the difference, and state the risk tolerance
$1/A$.

**★★6.** In the demonstration the EVSI at cost $20$ is exactly $0.0000$ for every
imperfect survey, while at cost $48$ it ranges from $1.05$ to $8.94$. Explain both
facts with reference to the conditional means in the table.

**★★7.** The EVPI at cost $20$ is $0.5831$ and at cost $48$ it is $11.2508$. Explain
the increase in terms of where the decision lies relative to the break-even point,
and state what a decision maker should conclude about buying information.

## ★★★ Research-oriented

**★★★8.** Design a value-of-information study for a monitoring network around a
storage site. State the actions, the states, the loss function, the candidate network
designs, and how you would compute and report the expected value of sample
information for each. State one way the study could be wrong even if the arithmetic
is correct.
